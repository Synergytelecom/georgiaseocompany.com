How Google Picks the Map Pack in Gainesville

Three things decide a map seat: whether your primary category matches what the customer typed, how close you sit to where they're standing, and what your review history looks like this month. Category is the one most owners get wrong.

A Gainesville business filed under a broad marketing or contractor label starts behind one filed under the exact service, before reviews enter the picture at all.

Recency carries more weight here than most owners expect. A profile holding a stack of reviews from four years ago, with nothing since, gives up seats to a competitor whose smaller stack arrived this spring.
