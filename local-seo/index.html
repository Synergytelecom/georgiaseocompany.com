What Local SEO Actually Is

Local SEO is a different contest from regular organic ranking. When someone searches for a service near them, Google shows a set of three map pack listings above the standard blue links, each pulled straight from a Google Business Profile rather than a webpage. Winning that block runs on its own rules: how close your business sits to the searcher, how well your listed category matches the search, and how your review count and recency stack up against the competitors shown beside you.

A page can rank first in the organic results below the map and still lose most of the clicks to whichever three businesses hold that block, because the phone number sits right on the map card and never sends the searcher to a website at all. That is why local seo services are scoped around the Business Profile first and the website second, the reverse order of how a national SEO campaign gets built, and it matches how someone running a local seo near me search actually behaves: they want a name and a phone number they can call right now, from a business they can be visited or hired by, not a website to read first.

What's Included in Local SEO

A local seo company engagement covers the pieces that actually move map pack position:

  • Google Business Profile setup and cleanup. Correct category, complete service area, accurate hours, and a full photo set.
  • Review strategy. A system for asking recent customers to leave reviews, without buying, faking, or incentivizing them.
  • Citation building and cleanup. Matching name, address, and phone data across the directories Google cross checks against your profile.
  • Location pages. Pages built for each city or service area you cover, written for the person searching, not stuffed with repeated keywords.
  • Map pack tracking. Rank checks pulled from inside the town you are targeting, since map pack position shifts by searcher location.
  • Competitor profile audits. A look at the categories, photos, and review pace of whoever currently holds your target spots.

Who Local SEO Is For

Does most of your new business already come from someone typing your service plus a town name into a phone standing nearby? That is the exact search this work is built to win: a restaurant, a repair shop, a clinic, a contractor working a defined service radius, anywhere the buyer decides based on who is closest and who looks trustworthy right now.

It fits less well for a business selling nationwide with no physical service area, since the map pack itself does not apply to that kind of search. A thin or negative review history is not disqualifying on its own, but it does need fixing as part of the plan rather than papered over with technical tweaks elsewhere.

Frequently asked questions

Do directories like Yelp or Angi matter for local SEO?

Yes, indirectly. Google cross-checks your name, address, and phone data against other directories when it decides how much to trust your Business Profile, so inconsistent listings on sites like Yelp or Angi can work against you even though those sites are not where the map pack itself lives.

How many locations or service areas can local SEO cover?

As many as your business actually serves. Each city or service area typically needs its own page and, if you have multiple physical locations, its own Google Business Profile with distinct local signals.

Do reviews really affect map pack ranking?

Review count and recency are one of the handful of signals that move map pack position, alongside proximity to the searcher and how well your category matches the search. A profile that stopped collecting reviews years ago tends to lose ground to competitors still adding new ones.

Can you fix a Google Business Profile that got suspended?

Suspensions usually trace back to a category, name, or verification issue, and fixing them starts with diagnosing which one applies to your case. We look at the specific suspension reason before promising any particular outcome.

How fast does local SEO show results compared to other SEO work?

Profile and citation fixes can move map pack position faster than organic content typically ranks, sometimes within weeks rather than months, though the exact timeline depends on how competitive your town and category already are.
