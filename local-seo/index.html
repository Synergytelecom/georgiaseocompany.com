What Local SEO Is

Local SEO is the work of ranking a business inside the map pack, the three listings that sit above the regular results when someone searches for a service near them. It is built for a business whose customers pick whoever is closest and looks active right now. The work is Google Business Profile setup, review activity, citation accuracy, and location pages, not the organic ranking signals alone.

Local SEO is a different contest from regular organic ranking. When someone searches for a service near them, Google shows a set of three map pack listings above the standard blue links, each pulled straight from a Google Business Profile rather than a webpage. Winning that block has its own rules: how close you are to the searcher, whether your listed category matches what they typed, and how your review count and recency compare to the businesses shown beside you.

A page can rank first in the organic results below the map and still lose most of the clicks to whichever three businesses hold that block, because the phone number sits right on the map card and the searcher never needs a website at all. That is why local seo services put the Business Profile first and the website second, the reverse of how a national SEO campaign gets built. It also matches how someone running a local seo near me search behaves: they want a name and a phone number they can call right now, not a website to read first.

What Is Included in Local SEO

A local seo company engagement covers the pieces that move map pack position:

  • Google Business Profile setup and cleanup. Correct category, complete service area, accurate hours, and a full photo set.
  • Review strategy. A system for asking recent customers to leave reviews, without buying, faking, or incentivizing them.
  • Citation building and cleanup. Matching name, address, and phone data across the directories Google cross checks against your profile.
  • Location pages. Pages built for each city or service area you cover, written for the person searching, not stuffed with repeated keywords.
  • Map pack tracking. Rank checks pulled from inside the town you are targeting, since map pack position shifts by searcher location.
  • Competitor profile audits. A look at the categories, photos, and review pace of whoever currently holds your target spots.

Who Local SEO Is For

Does most of your new business come from someone nearby, typing your service plus a town name into a phone? Restaurants, repair shops, clinics, and contractors working a defined service radius all live on that search, because the buyer picks whoever is closest and looks trustworthy right now.

It fits less well for a business selling nationwide with no physical service area, since the map pack itself does not apply to that kind of search. A thin or negative review history is not disqualifying on its own, but it does need fixing as part of the plan rather than papered over with technical tweaks elsewhere.

Our Local SEO Process

Local visibility work follows a set order, because cleaning up citations before the profile itself is correct just points them all at the wrong record:

  1. Profile audit. We check your current category, service areas, completeness, and review pace against the businesses beating you in the map pack.
  2. Citation cleanup. We fix mismatched or duplicate listings that confuse which business record Google should trust.
  3. Build out location pages. We add or repair the pages your website needs for each town or service area you work.
  4. Review pace. We put a simple system in place so recent reviews keep arriving instead of stalling out after a launch push.

Then we track map pack position by town every month and report exactly where it moved.

What the First 90 Days Look Like

The first 90 days follow a rough shape, though the pace depends on how much work the profile and citations need.

Weeks One Through Three: Diagnosis

The first two to three weeks are mostly diagnosis and cleanup: correcting the Google Business Profile category if it is off, fixing service areas, and finding the duplicate or mismatched citations that confuse which listing Google should trust. None of it shows up as a ranking change yet, but skipping it means building on a foundation that is still shifting underneath.

Weeks Four Through Eight: Building

Weeks four through eight are where location pages get built or repaired and a review request system goes in place, if one was not already running. This is also when the first map pack movement tends to show up, since profile and citation fixes can move faster than new content ranks.

Weeks Eight Through Twelve: Steady State

Weeks eight through twelve are steadier: reviews keep arriving on a set pace, location pages keep getting added for any town not yet covered, and the first full monthly report lands with a baseline to compare against.

None of this is a promise about where rankings will sit by day 90. A crowded town takes longer than an empty one, and a business starting from a suspended or badly mismatched profile has more cleanup ahead of it than one that just needs reviews and a few missing pages. The order does not change: profile first, pages second, review pace after that.

Frequently asked questions

Do directories like Yelp or Angi matter for local SEO?

Yes, indirectly. Google cross-checks your name, address, and phone data against other directories when it decides how much to trust your Business Profile, so inconsistent listings on sites like Yelp or Angi can work against you even though those sites are not where the map pack itself lives.

How many locations or service areas can local SEO cover?

As many as your business serves. Each city or service area typically needs its own page and, if you have multiple physical locations, its own Google Business Profile with distinct local signals.

Do reviews really affect map pack ranking?

Review count and recency are one of the handful of signals that move map pack position, alongside proximity to the searcher and how well your category matches the search. A profile that stopped collecting reviews years ago tends to lose ground to competitors still adding new ones.

Can you fix a Google Business Profile that got suspended?

Suspensions usually trace back to a category, name, or verification issue, and fixing them starts with diagnosing which one applies to your case. We look at the specific suspension reason before promising any particular outcome.

How fast does local SEO show results compared to other SEO work?

Profile and citation fixes can move map pack position faster than organic content typically ranks, sometimes within weeks rather than months, though the exact timeline depends on how competitive your town and category already are.
