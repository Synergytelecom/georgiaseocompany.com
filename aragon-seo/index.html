The Profile Fields That Decide an Aragon Ranking

Four settings do most of the work on a Google Business Profile in a market this size, and the rest is fine tuning next to them:

  • The leading category. It sets what you're eligible to appear for. Most owners choose it during the first hour of setup and never open the field again, which is why checking it is worth a few minutes every month.
  • The service area. Drawn along the routes your trucks really run, which for many businesses here means Rockmart and the Polk County roads first, Cedartown next, and Rome or Taylorsville only if the work genuinely goes there.
  • Reviews, on a rhythm. Asked for out loud while the invoice is still in your hand and the finished work is still in view, on every job, because a Polk County customer who has used you for years will do it right there and forget by supper.
  • Photos and hours. A profile carrying this month's work and this week's hours reads as a business that is open, and Google treats it that way. Hours that lie cost you a review nobody can delete.

That's the standing work behind profile management, and it's most of what separates the businesses inside the three cards from the ones sitting underneath them.

Frequently asked questions

Should we target Rockmart instead of Aragon, since it is the bigger town?

Target both, in that order, starting with the town your address sits in. The Aragon searches are cheaper to win and they convert well because the searcher has already decided they want somebody close, and a Rockmart page added on top of that has a stronger site holding it up.

How much of Polk County can one listing cover?

As far as your crew can drive and still finish well, because coverage that outruns your capacity turns up in the reviews before it turns up anywhere else. Coverage should follow the jobs you actually take, so the towns already sending you work get pages first and the rest get added as the routes spread.

We already have a website we like. Does it have to be replaced?

Usually not. Plenty of accounts start with a profile rebuild and a few new pages added to the site that already exists, and a full build only earns its cost when the old one is too slow or too broken to fix.

How often would anybody from your office actually come to Aragon?

Whenever a meeting is worth the short drive down from Rome. We come to you for the first one, and after that the monthly reporting runs on a call unless something needs a table and a laptop.
