What Is Custom Software Development?

Custom software development means building a tool around the way one business works, instead of bending the business to fit an app made for everyone. For a service business that's usually a small, sharp tool: a quote calculator, a booking form, a bot that sends each new lead to your phone, or an AI receptionist.

Off-the-shelf software covers the common jobs well, and we use it where it fits. A custom tool earns its keep in the gaps between those apps, where a lead gets copied by hand, a quote waits until evening, or a call rolls to voicemail.

Custom Tools We Build for Service Businesses

Every tool on this list is one we've built:

  • Instant quote tools. A customer picks the rooms and adds photos on your website, a price worked out from your own price list comes back by text, and the follow-up runs from your CRM.

AI Receptionist: Who Answers When You Can't?

An AI receptionist is an AI agent on your phone line. It answers while you're on a job or asleep, takes the caller's message and sends it to you, so the call turns into a lead instead of a voicemail.

It works from the facts you approve, such as your services, your towns and your hours, and takes a message for anything past them. Our Always Answered plan includes an AI agent that answers your calls and texts 24/7, and we also build agents on their own for a business that needs a front desk.

AI Automation: Where AI Helps and Where It Doesn't

AI is good at answering a call. The texts your customers read should be words you've approved, so most of the workflow automation behind a service business is better run on plain rules, with AI in the one or two steps where it helps.

So our automations run on rules you sign off on:

  • A new lead sends you an instant alert and gets a reply you approved ahead of time.
  • A missed call gets a text back.
  • A finished job gets a review request.
  • An open quote gets a follow-up text.

Each message is written once, approved by you, and sent the same way every time.

Business Automation That Connects the Software You Already Use

Business automation is mostly plumbing. The lead from your website, your Google profile or your phone goes into one CRM, then from the CRM into your job software, like Jobber, without anyone retyping it. When a program you use lets outside software connect, we can usually wire it in, and when one doesn't, we tell you at the start.

Connector apps like Zapier handle some of this, and sometimes they're the right answer. A custom connection makes sense when the steps get too involved for them.

Do You Need a Phone App or a Web App?

Most tools a service business needs run as a web app. It opens from your website or from a link you text, it works on any phone with a web browser, and nobody downloads anything. The quote and booking tools above work that way.

A phone app from the App Store or Google Play pays off when customers open it every week, the way they open an ordering or loyalty app. Few service customers do that, so for a quote, a booking or a question, a web app reaches them faster.

How Does a Custom Tool Get Built?

  1. A conversation. What the tool does, who uses it and what it connects to.
  2. A private preview. A working version at a private link, behind a password, for you to try.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between custom software and an off-the-shelf app?

An off-the-shelf app is built for thousands of businesses at once, so you adjust to it. Custom software is built for one business, so it follows your prices, your services and your steps. Most businesses run both, with custom tools filling the gaps between the apps they already use.

Do my customers have to download anything?

Not for a web tool. A quote or booking tool opens from your website or from a link you text, and it works in the web browser on the phone they already have.

Will a custom tool work with my CRM and job software?

Usually, as long as that software lets outside tools connect to it. Leads can go into your CRM and on into job software like Jobber, and we check the connection before the build starts.

Have a Job That Needs Its Own Tool?

Tell us the job: what eats your time, where leads slip away, or what you wish your website did. We'll tell you whether an app you can already buy covers it or it needs a custom tool, and what that tool would take.
