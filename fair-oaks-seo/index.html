Why a Fair Oaks Address Confuses Google

Mail through this part of Cobb County reads Marietta far more often than it reads Fair Oaks, and directories copy whatever they find first. That gap lands on three fields, and those three decide who ever sees your business at all:

  • Primary category. Nine more can sit underneath it, but the one on top sets which Cobb County searches you qualify for. It usually gets chosen in a hurry at setup and then sits untouched for years.
  • Service area. Drawn from the roads you cover in an ordinary week, which for a lot of trades here is a wedge running from Smyrna up past Kennesaw. A coverage claim stretched over four counties reads as noise, to Google and to the person looking at it.
  • Name, address and phone. One version of each, matching everywhere Google checks. Once Marietta and Fair Oaks are both circulating, directories multiply the split quietly, and the repair costs more than doing it right would have.

Distance from the searcher counts for a great deal in a county this dense. Two phones three miles apart get the same three cards back in a different order, which is why coverage gets set from real work and revisited when the work moves. That is the spine of our profile management.
