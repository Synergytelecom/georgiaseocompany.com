What a Top 3 Map Pack Spot Is Worth to You

Start with where that spot sits. On most local searches the map pack sits above every organic result on the page, with paid ads or an AI-generated summary sometimes above it.

Three signals decide who holds the top three: how close you are to the searcher, how well your Google Business Profile category matches what they typed, and how many recent reviews you have next to other local businesses.

Do the Math Yourself

So what is that spot worth to you? We will not hand you a percentage or a dollar figure, because we do not know your market. You bring the numbers and we do the math with you.

  • Count how many people search for your main service in your town each month. Your Business Profile insights or a keyword tool will tell you.
  • Estimate what share of them tap a map card instead of scrolling past it.
  • Multiply that share by how often you close a call, then by what an average job pays.

That number is in your dollars, not an industry average.

The Map Pack Runs Its Own Contest

You do not win that spot by pushing a keyword up the organic list. The map pack runs its own contest on its own signals. A business that holds its main service terms there gets calls that never touch its website, because the map card carries the phone number. How We Work: Audit, Build, Rank, Report

The process is the same whether you are starting from nothing or fixing a site that used to rank and stopped.

  • Audit. We pull your current rankings, your Google Business Profile health, your site speed, and your competitors' map pack positions. Nothing gets touched until we know what is broken.
  • Build. If the site is losing the visitors it gets, we design or rebuild it around what Google rewards: clear service pages, local proof, fast load times, and a phone number nobody has to hunt for.

  • Report. On a set schedule you get numbers, not activity: where you rank, which way it moved, and what we changed to move it.

How to Tell a Working SEO Firm From a Churn Shop

Before you hire an SEO firm, ask a few pointed questions and watch the answer. What gets optimized this month? Which keywords are the target? What happens to your rankings the month after you stop paying?

A search engine optimization company that is doing the work answers in specifics: named pages, named keywords, a monthly report you can check against your own analytics. A churn shop answers in fog, and none of it is checkable.

The other tell shows up when a client wants to leave. An agency that built something hands it over: Google Business Profile access, the site, the citation logins. An agency running on churn makes leaving hard on purpose. Ask who owns the accounts before you sign anything.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SEO cost for a Georgia business?

It depends on your market and how far behind your rankings and website are. Our SEO pricing page breaks down current Georgia market ranges, so you can see the numbers before you ever talk to us.

How long before we see results?

SEO moves in months, not days. In our experience, movement in rankings and calls shows up within three to six months, and it keeps building from there as the site and the Google Business Profile earn more trust. Anyone promising first page rankings in a couple of weeks is selling something that does not hold up.

What makes local SEO different from regular SEO?

National SEO chases organic rankings for broad keywords. Local SEO is won largely in the map pack, which runs on different signals: proximity to the searcher, Google Business Profile category and completeness, and review volume and recency. A page that ranks nationally can still lose the map pack to a closer competitor with fewer reviews.

Is web design part of your SEO service, or a separate cost?

It is part of the same process, not a bolt-on. Rankings that point at a slow or confusing site waste the traffic they earn. When your site is the bottleneck, we rebuild it or rework what you have inside the same plan, instead of sending you to a separate vendor.
