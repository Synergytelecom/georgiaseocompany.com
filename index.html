What a Top 3 Map Pack Spot Is Actually Worth

Start with the mechanics. In most local searches, the map pack sits above every traditional organic result on the page, sometimes with paid ads or an AI-generated summary above that. Three signals move a business in or out of that top three: proximity to the searcher, how well its Google Business Profile category matches what they typed, and how many recent reviews it has compared to the businesses beside it.

What is a map pack spot actually worth to your business? We will not hand you a percentage or a dollar figure, because we do not know your market. You supply the numbers, we supply the arithmetic. Start with how many people search for your core service in your town each month, a number you can pull from your Business Profile insights or a keyword tool. Estimate the share of those clicks the three map pack cards pull versus everything below them. Multiply that slice by your close rate, then by what an average job is worth, and you have the real size of the prize, in your own numbers.

That spot does not come from moving a keyword up out of the organic results. The map pack runs its own contest on its own signals, separate from organic rankings below it. A business holding its core service terms in the pack gets calls that never touch the website, because the phone number sits on the map card. How to Tell a Real SEO Firm From a Churn Shop

Before you hire an SEO firm, ask a few pointed questions and watch the answer. What exactly gets optimized this month, which keywords are the target, and how will you know it happened? What happens to your rankings the month after you stop paying? A real search engine optimization company answers in specifics: named pages, named keywords, a monthly report you can check against your own analytics. That is internet marketing done in the open. A churn shop answers in fog: general talk, no specifics, nothing you can check.

The other tell shows up when a client wants to leave. An agency doing real work hands over what it built: Google Business Profile access, the site, the citation logins. An agency running on churn makes leaving hard on purpose. Ask who owns the accounts before you sign anything.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SEO cost for a Georgia business?

It depends on your market and how much work your current rankings and website need. We break down current Georgia market ranges on our SEO pricing page so you can see real numbers before you ever talk to us.

How long before we see results?

SEO moves in months, not days. In our experience, most Georgia businesses start seeing real movement in rankings and calls within three to six months, and results keep building from there as the site and the Google Business Profile earn more trust. Anyone promising first page rankings in a couple of weeks is selling something that does not hold up.

What makes local SEO different from regular SEO?

National SEO chases organic rankings for broad keywords. Local SEO is won largely in the map pack, which runs on different signals: proximity to the searcher, Google Business Profile category and completeness, and review volume and recency. A page that ranks nationally can still lose the map pack to a closer competitor with fewer reviews.

Is web design part of your SEO service, or a separate cost?

It is part of the same process, not a bolt-on. Rankings that point to a slow or confusing website waste the traffic they earn, so when your site is holding you back we build a new one or rework what you have as part of the plan instead of sending you to a separate vendor.
