Frequently asked questions

Do we need a Marietta office address to rank here?

You need a real address or a real service area, and if you already operate in Marietta you have one. Ours being in Rome is beside the point, because Google measures the distance between your business and the customer, not between you and your agency.

How long does local SEO take in Marietta?

Profile fixes such as the primary category and the service area can show inside a few weeks. Reviews and history take longer, so a competitive Cobb County phrase is usually a one to two quarter project. Searches that carry the town name tend to move before the plain ones do.

We already rank for our own name in Marietta. Is that enough?

Name searches already belong to you, and they come from people who heard about you somewhere else. The volume sits in searches that pair a service with a town, where the customer has nobody in mind yet and picks from three cards. Category, distance to the searcher and review dates decide those, so a business can own its name and still sit off that list.

What does the monthly work cover for a Marietta client?

A market check for changes in keyword demand, profile edits across categories, services, service areas, photos and posts, page work on the site, review requests going out on finished jobs, and a written report on a set date with your rankings town by town. Eric Kirby does each of those himself.
