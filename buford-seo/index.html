Buford Runs the Usual Pattern Backward

In most of these fourteen towns, web design searches hold the higher bar. Buford is the opposite.

Three Terms Tied at 62, and the Design Searches Lower

Buford is home to the Mall of Georgia and straddles the Gwinnett-Hall county line, with roughly 18,600 people. In July 2026 it posted one of the three highest review bars in this fourteen-city band: three of the six search terms shared an identical 62-review leader. What makes Buford's version of a high bar unusual is which searches hold it.

SEO company, SEO services, and Local SEO, three separate terms, all returned the exact same leader review count of 62. Web design company and web designer both came in lower, at 48. Digital marketing agency sat lowest of all, at 29.

A High Bar, Held by the Terms You Would Expect to Be Easy

In several other towns in this band, the plainer SEO-labeled searches turned up the softest numbers and the design-labeled searches turned up the toughest. Buford flips that. Here the businesses ranking for SEO company, SEO services, and Local SEO have built the deepest review history in town, while the web design and digital marketing searches are the more open lane.

The flip holds across three separate terms on each side, which is more than one unusual search could produce on its own. It also means a single citywide figure describes Buford poorly. The 62 and the 29 sit on two different starting lines.
