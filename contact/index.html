Reach Out

The fastest way to reach Georgia SEO Company is a call to (762) 999-RANK. However you get here, the first conversation runs the same way, and the next section walks through it.

What Happens After You Reach Out

It goes the same way every time. First we ask a few questions about the business and the market it competes in. Then we run a visibility audit: where you rank now, how healthy the Google Business Profile is, and a look at the website itself.

You get a read on where things stand before any commitment or contract enters the conversation. If the numbers do not justify the work, you hear that too, instead of a version dressed up to close a sale. Nothing in that first conversation obligates you past hearing the answer.

When It Makes Sense to Move Forward

If it makes sense to move forward, the next steps get scoped to what the audit found: which pages need work, which parts of the Google Business Profile are costing you map pack position, and what a realistic path looks like from there.

When It Does Not Make Sense Yet

If it does not make sense yet, we say so. Sometimes the site or the market is not ready. Sometimes the fix is one you can do yourself, and we would rather tell you that than invent a reason to sell something.

Whoever walks you through that audit is also who does the work if you move forward. There is no rotating account team. Our about page goes into that setup in more depth.

Where We're Based

Georgia SEO Company is based in Rome, Georgia, and works with businesses across Northwest Georgia and the rest of the state from there. No street address is published here, the same way a service area business is listed everywhere else on this site. Rome is the home base, and the work gets done wherever the client is.

That territory covers the full state, not just the towns closest to Rome. A business further out gets the same audit and the same reporting. What changes is drive time to an occasional in-person meeting.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve businesses outside Rome?

Yes. Rome is home base, but the territory reaches out through Northwest Georgia and across the rest of the state too. The only difference further out is drive time to an in-person meeting, not the standard of the work.

Is the visibility audit actually free, with no obligation to continue?

Yes, entirely. We check where you currently rank, your Google Business Profile, and your website, all before any contract or commitment enters the conversation. What you do with the findings afterward is up to you.

What should I have ready before reaching out?

Your website address helps most, plus a link to your Google Business Profile if you have one. Beyond that, tell us what feels broken: fewer calls than last year, a competitor that keeps beating you to the map pack, whatever prompted the search.

Will I talk to a salesperson, or the person who actually does the work?

The person who does the work. Nothing passes through a salesperson first. Whoever runs your audit and builds your pages also picks up the phone, with no hand off to someone new partway through. More on that background is on our about page.
