What Happens After You Reach Out

It goes the same way every time. We ask a few questions about the business and the market it competes in, then we run the audit: where you rank in your market today, how healthy the Google Business Profile is, and what the website is doing with the traffic it already gets.

You get that read before any contract. If the work isn't worth what it would cost you, we'll say so, and sometimes the fix is one you can do yourself, which you'll also hear. Hearing the answer is the whole of what that first conversation commits you to.

If It Makes Sense to Move Forward

The next steps get scoped to what the audit found: which pages need work, which parts of the Google Business Profile are costing you map pack position, and what a realistic path looks like from there. From then on those same checks run every month. We watch your market for changes in keyword demand, optimize the site and the profile against what we find, and send you a report showing where your rankings went and what we changed.

Whoever walks you through that audit is also who does the work if you move forward. Our about page goes into that setup in more depth.

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve businesses outside Rome?

Yes. Rome is home base, but the territory reaches out through Northwest Georgia and across the rest of the state too. The only difference further out is drive time to an in-person meeting, not the standard of the work.

Is the visibility audit actually free, with no obligation to continue?

Yes, entirely. We check where you currently rank, your Google Business Profile, and your website, all before any contract or commitment enters the conversation. What you do with the findings afterward is up to you.

What should I have ready before reaching out?

Your website address helps most, plus a link to your Google Business Profile if you have one. Beyond that, tell us what feels broken: fewer calls than last year, a competitor that keeps beating you to the map pack, whatever prompted the search.

Will I talk to a salesperson, or the person who actually does the work?

The person who does the work. Nothing passes through a salesperson first. Whoever runs your audit and builds your pages also picks up the phone, with no hand off to someone new partway through. More on that background is on our about page.
