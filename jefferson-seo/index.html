Frequently asked questions

How long until a new Jefferson website starts ranking?

A few weeks for the searches that name the town and the service, longer for anything a directory or a metro competitor already holds. Structure and page depth decide most of it, which is why the wireframe stage matters more than the color palette.

Do reviews on directory sites help my Google ranking?

Not directly. Google weighs the reviews on your own Google Business Profile, so that's where the routine belongs. Consistent name, address, and phone data on those directories does matter, because Google cross-checks it, and inconsistent records quietly cost trust.

What should I look for in an SEO agency?

A clear description of how they choose target terms, what gets reported, and who touches your account. Ask what month six looks like and expect specifics. A pitch that names your future ranking, vague pricing, and work you can't verify in your own Search Console are the three reasons to keep looking.
