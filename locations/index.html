Pick the Town, Not the State

Local search is decided town by town, and a business ranking first in one town can sit off the map entirely in the next one over. That is not a flaw in the measurement, it is how proximity works: Google puts the searcher at the center and sorts by who is close, relevant, and trusted, in that order. A statewide average would hide the only number that matters to a business owner, which is where they stand in the towns their crews actually drive to.

So every market gets its own page, and the ranking work gets measured the same way in each one. The pages below carry what is specific to that market: the county, the corridors, the population, and what we measured there.
