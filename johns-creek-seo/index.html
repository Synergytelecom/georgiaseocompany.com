How a Johns Creek Map Search Gets Decided

Four signals do most of the deciding, and none of them live on your website:

  • Primary category. One primary, up to nine more underneath it. The primary sets which Johns Creek searches you are eligible for at all, and most owners pick it once during setup and leave it alone for years.
  • Where the phone is standing. Search from Medlock Bridge Road, then search again from State Bridge Road, and the order moves, because Google weighs how close each business sits to the person searching. Cross the Chattahoochee and you are into Gwinnett County, where the field of names changes again.
  • Service area. Drawn to the roads you actually drive, whether that is Johns Creek alone or Alpharetta, Duluth and Suwanee with it. An honest radius outperforms one stretched across half the metro.
  • Review dates. Recency carries more weight than the total. Ten reviews from this year usually outrun thirty that stopped arriving three summers ago.

The category and the service area are quick levers, and their effect usually lands within weeks. Review history is the slow half, since it only builds on finished jobs, which is why the two get worked on different clocks.
