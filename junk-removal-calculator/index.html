How the Junk Removal Pricing Calculator Works

JunkIQ is free and unlimited for junk removal businesses anywhere in the US. It runs in Telegram, so it opens your camera and needs nothing else to install.

  1. Send the ZIP code for the job.
  2. Send up to 15 photos of the junk, right from your camera.
  3. Type GO. You get a low, middle and high price with a recommended price to quote, plus the load size, crew hours and disposal costs.

How to Price Junk Removal Jobs

A junk removal price has to cover what the job costs you before it can make you money. JunkIQ works through the same costs on every job:

  • Disposal: the weight of the load at the dump fee for the ZIP code, plus surcharges for items like mattresses, tires, appliances and TVs
  • Labor: hours and cost for a crew of one, two or three, with the crew size that fits the load
  • Job conditions: stairs, long carries and tight access that add time
  • Salvage: items worth enough to offset part of the cost
  • Flags: hazardous items are flagged for a specialist instead of priced

The price is an estimate from your photos. You check the job and set the final price.

Frequently asked questions

Is this junk removal calculator really free?

Yes. JunkIQ is free and unlimited for junk removal businesses anywhere in the US, and there is no card to enter.

How much should I charge for a junk removal job?

That depends on the load, your crew and the dump fees where you work. Send JunkIQ the ZIP code and photos and it gives you a low, middle and high price for that job, with a recommended price. The final price is always yours to set.

Can I get a junk removal estimate from photos?

Yes. Send up to 15 photos of the job. If the photos are not clear enough to size the load, JunkIQ tells you what other shots it needs.

Do I need to buy junk removal software to use this?

No. JunkIQ is not a paid software plan. It runs in Telegram on your phone, so all you need is the free Telegram app.

Does it work outside Georgia?

Yes. It works anywhere in the US. It uses disposal fees for the ZIP code of the job when we have them and regional rates when we do not.

Can a homeowner use this to price their own junk?

JunkIQ is built for junk removal businesses pricing jobs for their customers, so sign-up asks for your business name.
