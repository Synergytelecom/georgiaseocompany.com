Suwanee Is the Easy Case, With One Exception

Suwanee, a Gwinnett County city of roughly 23,100 people, posted a median leader review count of 9, one of the three lowest in this fourteen-city measurement, from a single location-set capture taken July 27, 2026.

SEO company, SEO services, and Local SEO bunch together at 9. Web design company and web designer come in just under that, both at 8, five of six terms packed inside a single point of each other. Digital marketing agency breaks away from the rest entirely, landing at 64, and it does not belong folded into the average. That one search is an established climb, not an open field like the other five.

Why a Single-Digit Bar Is Worth Moving On Now

An 8 or 9 review leader is barely established. Asking every customer, every time, is usually enough on its own to close that gap within two or three normal months. Which is also the catch. If the leader is easy to overtake, so are you, by whoever comes next.

The one term that broke the pattern, digital marketing agency, is worth noting for a different reason. A business whose customers are more likely to search that specific phrase should not expect the same easy runway the other five terms show.

Why the Marketing-Agency Search Behaves Differently Than the Rest

Digital marketing agency, as a category and as a search phrase, tends to be claimed by businesses positioned more broadly than a single-town operation, often serving several nearby cities under one listing rather than Suwanee specifically. That broader positioning is usually how a business ends up with a review count like 64 while five neighboring searches in the same city sit at 8 or 9. It is a different kind of competitor, not a preview of where the other five terms are headed.

A Suwanee business built around ordinary local services rarely needs to compete on that particular phrase at all. The plainer terms, SEO company, SEO services, Local SEO, web designer, and web design company, are the ones a Suwanee-based service business is most likely to be searched under, and every one of those sits in single digits.

Reading the 64 as Suwanee's bar, instead of reading it as one broadly positioned outlier, is the most common way to misjudge this particular market.

Who This Fits in Suwanee
