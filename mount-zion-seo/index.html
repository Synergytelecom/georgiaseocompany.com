Frequently asked questions

Is a city the size of Mount Zion worth its own page?

Almost always. The phrase carries little competition because nobody in Carrollton has bothered writing for it, and a page aimed at one small city usually earns its keep faster than anything broader on the same site.

We live here but most of our jobs are in Carrollton. Where does the business get listed?

List it where you operate from and let the service area do the reaching. Owners get tempted to move the pin into Carrollton, and that is the one shortcut Google actively polices, since verification runs against the real address.

How much of this happens in person?

The first meeting does, at your place. Rome sits a little over an hour north by way of Cedartown, and the work after that runs on calls unless there is something worth spreading across a table.
