Seo company, seo services, and local seo each returned a leader at 26 reviews. Web design company, web designer, and digital marketing agency each returned a leader at 50. Three and three, an even split, with no term landing anywhere in between.

Reading a Market That Divides This Cleanly

A split this even is its own kind of information. It says the businesses ranking for the plainer SEO-labeled searches in Duluth have built roughly half the review history of the ones ranking for the design and marketing-labeled searches, consistently across all three terms on each side. That is not noise. Whatever category or positioning choice separates those two groups of businesses is producing a repeatable gap.

Either side of that split is a workable target. A 26-review leader is a shorter climb than a 50-review one, obviously, but neither number describes a market that has been locked down for years. Both are numbers a business asking for reviews on a normal schedule can realistically close.

One Category Choice Decides Which Three Searches You Can Even Win

A Google Business Profile carries one primary category, chosen by the business itself when the listing is set up. That single choice is part of why Duluth's six searches split so evenly into two groups of three. A business categorized as a marketing or web design service is eligible to compete for web design company, web designer, and digital marketing agency in a way a plainer local-service category is not, and the reverse is just as true.

Which matters, because Duluth's split does not necessarily reflect two entirely separate sets of businesses. In practice it is often the same handful of local agencies sorted onto one side or the other by a single field on their own profile. A business unsure which side it currently sits on can check its own category directly. That one setting is doing more work than most owners realize.

Getting it right, before anything else gets touched, is frequently the one fix that moves the most on a profile that already has some review history behind it.
