Profile Settings That Matter at the Foot of Fort Mountain

Most searches in a county this size end at three cards under a map, and Google fills all three from your profile. Four settings do the heavy lifting:

  • Primary category. Nine more can sit beneath it, and the one on top decides which Murray County searches you're eligible for at all.
  • Service area by road. The mountains east of town mean a circle takes in ridge and forest nobody drives through, so coverage follows 411 north to Eton, 76 west toward Dalton, and whatever you'll take up GA 52.
  • Hours you can stand behind. Wrong hours cost you twice in a county where people drive a while to reach town, once on the wasted trip and again in the review it produces.
  • Photos and posts with this season on them. A profile carrying recent work reads as a going concern, and February around here looks nothing like July.

All four get revisited on a schedule, since Google adds and retires categories quietly and competitors redraw their coverage without announcing it. The standing version of that work is our profile management service.
