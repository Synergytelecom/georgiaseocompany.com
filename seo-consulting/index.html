What SEO Consulting Is

SEO consulting is outside direction for a marketing team or agency that is already doing the SEO work itself. We advise, your team executes. It covers strategy and technical review, Google Business Profile checks, a priority roadmap, and regular working sessions where a team can ask specific questions. It fits a team with real marketing capacity but limited SEO depth, or one that inherited a strategy it is no longer sure makes sense.

Some businesses do not need an agency to run their SEO end to end. They already have a marketing person or a small in-house team doing the work, and what they need is an outside seo consultant to check direction, catch mistakes, and name the priorities they are too close to see. That is a different engagement than a full seo services contract, and we scope it differently.

The Specific Questions We Ask

A search engine optimization consultation is built around specific questions:

  • Is the content plan aimed at the right search terms?
  • Is the Google Business Profile set up correctly?
  • Is the site technically sound?
  • Where should limited time go next month?

We come in as an seo specialist advising your team, not as a replacement for it.

A Periodic Checkpoint, Not a Constant Presence

Consulting also works as a periodic checkpoint rather than a constant presence. A quarterly review can catch a category change or a technical regression before it shows up as a ranking drop.

What's Included in Consulting

A consulting engagement is scoped around your team's actual gaps, typically drawing from:

  • Strategy audit. A review of your current SEO plan, priorities, and where the time and budget are actually going.
  • Technical review. A check of site structure, speed, and on-page fundamentals your in-house team may not have the background to catch.
  • Google Business Profile review. Category, service area, and completeness checked against what actually moves map pack position.
  • Priority roadmap. A ranked list of what to fix first, built around impact rather than an arbitrary checklist.
  • Direct access. Regular check-ins where your team can ask specific questions as work comes up, instead of a single report handed off and forgotten.
  • Vendor evaluation. If you are considering hiring an agency for part of the work, a second opinion on the scope and pricing you are being offered.
  • Content calendar review. A check of what your team is planning to publish next, weighed against what actually has measurable search demand behind it.

Who Consulting Fits

Most teams we consult for look alike: one marketing person, or a small team, running SEO alongside four other jobs. The bottleneck is rarely effort. It is knowing which of the twenty things on the list to do this month. If that describes your situation, a consultant who can name the one or two priorities worth your limited time this month tends to be more useful than a full agency takeover.

It also fits an agency or web development shop that needs SEO expertise for a specific client project without hiring a full-time specialist. It is not a fit for a business that wants the work fully executed rather than directed; our standard seo services engagement covers that instead.

Why Our Direction Holds Up

We run our own client SEO work every month. The priorities we hand you come from what is moving rankings on accounts we manage right now, not from a playbook written once and never tested.

Current measured rankings for that work are public on our results page, which is also where you can check whether the direction we give holds up over time instead of just taking it on faith.

How Consulting Engagements Work

Consulting starts with what your team already has in place, since a plan built without that tends to repeat work you have already done:

  1. Discovery. We review your current site, rankings, Business Profile, and whatever strategy or plan already exists.
  2. Findings. We document specific gaps and opportunities, ranked by likely impact, not a generic audit template.
  3. Working sessions. We meet with your team to walk through findings and answer questions as your team plans the actual work.
  4. Ongoing check-ins. For engagements that continue past the initial audit, we set a regular cadence to review progress and adjust direction.

What a Findings Session Actually Covers

A findings session is not a slide deck read aloud. It starts with what we checked: current rankings for the terms that matter, the state of the Google Business Profile, and the site's technical health, each reported on its own instead of folded into a single score. Then we go through what is holding things back, in order of how much each one matters, not in the order we happened to find them.

The session also covers what is already working, since a team correcting course needs to know what to leave alone as much as what to fix. We name specific pages, keywords, and Google Business Profile settings, because a priority list your team cannot act on the next morning is not really a priority list. We leave time for questions that were not on the agenda, since the gap a team needs help with is not always the one that looked most urgent from outside.

What a team walks away with is a short, ordered list: what to do first, what can wait, and what we would check again at the next session to see whether the first items actually moved anything.

Frequently asked questions

How is SEO consulting different from your full SEO services?

Consulting gives your team direction, priorities, and specialist review while your team or another vendor executes the work. Full SEO services means we execute the work ourselves. Consulting can be an entry point that leads into full execution later, or an ongoing arrangement on its own, depending on what your team needs.

Can an SEO consultant work with our existing marketing agency?

Yes. We can review work being done by another vendor and give a second opinion on strategy, pricing, or technical decisions, without requiring that vendor relationship to end.

How long does an SEO consulting engagement last?

Consulting can be a single audit and roadmap session, or an ongoing check-in that continues for months as priorities shift. We scope the length around what your team actually needs.

Do you consult on Google Business Profile specifically?

Yes, and it is often the highest-impact part of a consulting engagement, since profile category and service area mistakes are common and usually straightforward to correct once identified.

Is SEO consulting a good fit for a business with no marketing staff?

Usually not on its own. Consulting assumes someone on your side can act on the direction given. A business with no one to execute the work is typically better served by a full SEO services engagement instead.
