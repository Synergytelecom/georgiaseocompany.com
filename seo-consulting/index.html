What an Outside SEO Consultant Brings

If your team already runs the SEO, we bring the direction. We review the site, the Google Business Profile, and what you're ranking for now, then hand your team a short ordered list of what to do next month. Your team executes, and an SEO consultant coming in this way answers four questions: whether the content plan is aimed at the right search terms, whether the profile is set up correctly, whether the site is technically sound, and where limited time goes next month.

Consulting only lands where somebody on your side can act on it. A business with no marketing staff gets more out of an engagement where we do the work, which is our SEO services page.

It also works as a periodic checkpoint rather than a constant presence. A quarterly review catches a category change or a technical regression while it's still a small correction.

What an SEO Consulting Engagement Includes

Our SEO consulting services get scoped around your team's gaps, drawn from six pieces:

  • Strategy audit. Where the time and the budget are going now, and what that spend is producing.
  • Technical review. Site structure, speed, and the on-page fundamentals a marketing generalist has no reason to have learned.
  • Google Business Profile review. Category, service area, and completeness, checked against what moves map pack position.
  • Priority roadmap. A ranked list of what to fix first, ordered by impact.
  • Vendor evaluation. A second opinion on the scope and price an agency has quoted you, with that relationship left intact either way.
  • Content calendar review. What your team plans to publish next, weighed against the terms your customers are actually typing.

Which Teams This Fits

Most teams we consult for look alike: one marketing person, or a small team, running SEO alongside four other jobs. The bottleneck is rarely effort.

It's knowing which of the twenty things on the list to do this month, and a consultant who can name the one or two worth the time you have is worth more to that team than a full agency takeover.

It fits an agency or a web development shop the same way, when a specific client project needs SEO depth and hiring a full-time SEO specialist for it makes no sense.

What an Ongoing Engagement Looks Like Month to Month

The first pass is discovery and findings: your site, your rankings, your Business Profile, and whatever plan already exists. After that an ongoing engagement settles into a cycle:

  1. Market check. We check your market for changes in keyword demand and review what's trending in other markets and on the sites competing with yours.
  2. Working session. We meet with your team and work through what those changes mean for next month's list.
  3. Follow-up. We check whether last month's items moved anything, and the list gets reordered around the answer.

The priorities we hand you come out of what's moving rankings on the accounts we run ourselves this month. The accounts we run have their rankings on our results page.

What a Search Engine Optimization Consultation Covers

A findings session starts with what we checked: current rankings for the terms that matter, the state of the Google Business Profile, and the site's technical health, each reported on its own line rather than folded into one score. Then we go through what's holding things back, ordered by how much each one is costing you.

We name specific pages, keywords, and profile settings, because a priority list your team can't act on the next morning is a summary. We name what's already working too, since a team correcting course needs to know what to leave alone.

Time gets left for the questions that weren't on the agenda, because the gap a team needs help with is often the one that looked minor from outside.

Everybody walks out with a short ordered list: what to do first, what can wait, and what we check again next session to see whether the first items moved anything.

Frequently asked questions

How is SEO consulting different from your full SEO services?

Consulting gives your team direction, priorities, and specialist review while your team or another vendor executes the work. Our full SEO services engagement means we execute the work ourselves. Consulting can be an entry point that leads into full execution later, or an ongoing arrangement on its own, depending on what your team needs.

Can an SEO consultant work with our existing marketing agency?

Yes. We can review work being done by another vendor and give a second opinion on strategy, pricing, or technical decisions, without requiring that vendor relationship to end.

How long does an SEO consulting engagement last?

Consulting can be a single audit and roadmap session, or an ongoing check-in that continues for months as priorities shift. We scope the length around what your team needs.

Do you consult on Google Business Profile specifically?

Yes, and it is often the highest-impact part of a consulting engagement, since profile category and service area mistakes are common and usually straightforward to correct once identified.

Is SEO consulting a good fit for a business with no marketing staff?

Usually not. Consulting assumes someone on your side can act on the direction given. A business with no one to execute the work is typically better served by a full SEO services engagement.
