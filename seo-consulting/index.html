What SEO Consulting Is

Some businesses do not need an agency to run their SEO end to end; they already have a marketing person or a small in-house team doing the work and need an outside seo consultant to check direction, catch mistakes, and set priorities they are too close to the day-to-day to see clearly. That is a different engagement than a full seo services contract, and we scope it differently.

A search engine optimization consultation is built around answering specific questions: is the content plan targeting the right search terms, is the Google Business Profile set up correctly, is the site's technical foundation actually sound, and where should limited time go next month. We come in as an seo specialist advising your team, not as a replacement for it.

This tends to fit teams with real marketing capacity but no deep SEO background, or teams that inherited a strategy from a past agency and are not sure whether it still makes sense. Consulting also works well as a periodic checkpoint rather than a constant presence: a quarterly review can catch a category change or a technical regression well before it shows up as a ranking drop your team notices too late.

What's Included

A consulting engagement is scoped around your team's actual gaps, typically drawing from:

  • Strategy audit. A review of your current SEO plan, priorities, and where the time and budget are actually going.
  • Technical review. A check of site structure, speed, and on-page fundamentals your in-house team may not have the background to catch.
  • Google Business Profile review. Category, service area, and completeness checked against what actually moves map pack position.
  • Priority roadmap. A ranked list of what to fix first, built around impact rather than an arbitrary checklist.
  • Direct access. Regular check-ins where your team can ask specific questions as work comes up, instead of a single report handed off and forgotten.
  • Vendor evaluation. If you are considering hiring an agency for part of the work, a second opinion on the scope and pricing you are being offered.

Who This Is For

This fits an in-house marketing team that owns SEO execution but wants an experienced seo consultant checking their direction periodically, and it fits a business owner doing the SEO work personally who wants a specialist to review it before committing more time to a plan that might be pointed the wrong way.

It also fits an agency or web development shop that needs SEO expertise for a specific client project without hiring a full-time specialist. It is not a fit for a business that wants the work fully executed rather than directed; that is what our standard seo services engagement covers instead. Teams stretched thin across several marketing responsibilities, with SEO as just one of many hats, tend to get the most out of a consultant who can name the one or two priorities worth their limited time this month.

How Consulting Engagements Work

Consulting starts with understanding what your team already has in place:

  1. Discovery. We review your current site, rankings, Business Profile, and whatever strategy or plan already exists.
  2. Findings. We document specific gaps and opportunities, ranked by likely impact, not a generic audit template.
  3. Working sessions. We meet with your team to walk through findings and answer questions as your team plans the actual work.
  4. Ongoing check-ins. For engagements that continue past the initial audit, we set a regular cadence to review progress and adjust direction.

Frequently asked questions

How is SEO consulting different from your full SEO services?

Consulting gives your team direction, priorities, and specialist review while your team or another vendor executes the work. Full SEO services means we execute the work ourselves. Some clients start with consulting and move to full execution later, or the reverse.

Can an SEO consultant work with our existing marketing agency?

Yes. We regularly review work being done by another vendor and give a second opinion on strategy, pricing, or technical decisions, without requiring that vendor relationship to end.

How long does an SEO consulting engagement last?

Some clients want a single audit and roadmap session. Others keep a recurring check-in going for months as priorities shift. We scope the length around what your team actually needs.

Do you consult on Google Business Profile specifically?

Yes, and it is often the highest-impact part of a consulting engagement, since profile category and service area mistakes are common and usually straightforward to correct once identified.

Is SEO consulting a good fit for a business with no marketing staff?

Usually not on its own. Consulting assumes someone on your side can act on the direction given. A business with no one to execute the work is typically better served by a full SEO services engagement instead.
