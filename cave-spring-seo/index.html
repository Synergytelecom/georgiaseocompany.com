Frequently asked questions

Is Cave Spring big enough for monthly SEO to be worth it?

For most trades and service businesses here, yes, because the cost of competing is low. A short field means a finished profile and one page written for the town carry more weight than the same effort would in a metro county, and the audit tells you before you spend anything.

We take jobs across the Alabama line. Should that go on the profile?

Only if you truly work there often enough to serve those customers well. Coverage should match your route sheet, and a service area padded with places you rarely reach dilutes the towns you actually want to rank in.

Should our listing read Cave Spring or Rome?

Whatever your license, your insurance certificate and your invoices already say, written the same way everywhere Google can look it up. Two versions of your address across different directories split the credit between them, and cleaning it up later takes far longer than doing it once.

How fast does anything change after the work starts?

Corrections to categories and coverage tend to surface within a few weeks. Reviews arrive on their own schedule since they follow closed jobs, so a phrase pairing your trade with the town usually takes three to six months, and broader terms take longer than that.
