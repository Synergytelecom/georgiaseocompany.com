The Suite Number Problem in Dunwoody Office Towers

A business in a Perimeter Center building has an address with a suite number, a shared lobby, and often a shared mail room. Google is strict about this. The listed address has to be a place a customer could actually reach you during your posted hours, suite included, and it cross-checks that whole line against the directories.

Two things go wrong routinely. The suite gets dropped from half the records, so the address reads two different ways depending on where Google looks. Or a coworking desk gets listed as a permanent office, which puts the profile a single complaint away from a suspension. Both are fixable before they cost you the listing, and both take a careful pass through the citation records.

The county line adds one more wrinkle.
