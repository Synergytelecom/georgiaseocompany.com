What the Monthly Local SEO Work Covers

Local SEO is scoped around your Google Business Profile and the towns you work in. Every month we work the profile category and the service area, keep a review routine running, keep your name, address, and phone consistent across the directories Google checks, and write a page for each town you serve.

Alongside that we check your market monthly for changes in keyword demand, optimize the profile and those pages against what we find, and send a report showing where you stand in the map pack, town by town.

The profile leads because of where the map pack sits. On a phone search pairing a trade with a town, those few cards sit above every organic result and each one carries a phone number, so a business holding a seat gets calls that never load a website.

Three things on the profile decide who gets shown: the category it's filed under, the service area it declares, and how recently it has collected reviews next to the other businesses in the same pack.

Underneath it sit the citation records Google cross-checks when it decides how much to trust a listing, and the town pages that give each market something of its own to rank.

Why Cheap Local SEO Packages Underdeliver

A local SEO package priced well under the bottom of that range buys less labor, and the arithmetic is easy to run. Writing a page for one city takes research and time, and a business serving eight or ten towns needs eight or ten of those pages written. A template with the city name swapped in reads like a template.

Asking recent customers for reviews on a consistent schedule takes somebody working that schedule every month. Cleaning up citation data across dozens of directories by hand takes hours.

At a price that can't cover that labor, the work leans on shortcuts: one generic page reused across every town, automated citation submissions with no cleanup behind them, or whatever review flow was already happening before the contract started. The map pack position is the first place the gap shows, since it runs on exactly the signals a shortcut skips.

Done properly, the position follows. One client we run this work for holds the top map pack spot in its home market on the searches that name both the service and the town, and the full write-up is on our proof page. Where the rest of our clients sit in their own map packs is on the results page.

Questions Worth Asking Before You Sign a Local SEO Contract

This is a narrower job than a full organic campaign, which makes it easier to check whether a proposal covers it. Four asks separate local SEO work from a package borrowing the name:

  • Ask how many city or service area pages are included. A single "our service areas" page and a written page per town are different deliverables, and the second one costs more to produce.
  • Ask which category goes on the Google Business Profile, and why that one. A vague answer here usually means nobody has looked at your trade yet.
  • Ask how often review requests go out. A one-time setup sends one wave and stops.
  • Ask how citation cleanup gets verified. A before and after from a past listing shows it happened.

A proposal answering all four in specifics is describing real local SEO work. One that answers in generalities is priced like it.

Frequently asked questions

How much does local SEO cost per month?

Single-market local SEO runs $300 to $1,500+ a month. That is the kind of engagement focused on map pack position rather than a broad organic campaign. A business chasing rankings across several towns or a competitive trade should expect to land higher in that band or move into the broader campaign ranges covered on our SEO pricing page.

What decides where in that range my own price lands?

Three things. How many towns need a page and a rank check of their own, how contested your trade already is in those towns, and what condition your Google Business Profile and citation records are in on day one. A suspended profile or a stack of duplicate listings is repair work before the monthly work starts.

What is actually included in a local SEO engagement at that price?

Google Business Profile management, citation building and cleanup across the directories Google checks against your listing, an ongoing routine for prompting fresh reviews, and location pages written for each town you serve. Skip any one of those pieces and the map pack position slips even when the rest looks fine.

Why is some local SEO so cheap?

At a price well under $300 a month, the budget cannot cover a person writing a city page, requesting reviews on a schedule, or cleaning up mismatched citation data by hand. The offer leans on templated pages and automated directory submissions. A report can list all of that as delivered work in a month where the map pack position never moves.

How is local SEO pricing different from a broader SEO campaign?

Local SEO is scoped around one market and the Google Business Profile that serves it, a narrower and usually less expensive job than an organic campaign spanning many keywords or towns. Our full SEO pricing page breaks out both bands side by side.
