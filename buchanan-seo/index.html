Profile Fields That Decide a Haralson County Search

Three cards under a map settle most searches in a county this size, and Google builds those cards out of your profile. Four fields carry nearly all of the weight:

  • The leading category. This is the field deciding which searches you can appear in at all. Google quietly adds and retires categories, so a setting that was right two years ago may be pointing at nothing today.
  • The service area. Set to the county you honestly cover, which for most Buchanan trades runs from Tallapoosa across to Waco and up toward Cedartown, plus whatever you would take across the Carroll County line.
  • Name, address and phone. One version of each, written identically everywhere Google looks them up. A second version fails to replace the first, it competes with it.
  • Reviews carrying this year's dates. Recency does more work than volume in a small county, and the dependable way to collect them is asking while the customer is still standing in front of the finished job.

All four get looked at every month rather than once, because a field that was correct in March has no obligation to still be correct in September. That standing work is what profile management comes down to in practice.
