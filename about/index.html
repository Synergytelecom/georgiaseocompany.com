Georgia SEO Company Is Led by Eric Kirby

Eric Kirby leads Georgia SEO Company from Rome, Georgia. He's spent more than twenty years in business technology, including hands-on work inside healthcare IT, where a wrong diagnosis costs a great deal more than a lost click and you're expected to get it right the first time.

One habit came over from that work. Find what's broken, fix it, then measure whether the fix held. That's the same loop we run on a client's rankings every month: check the market for changes in keyword demand, optimize the Google Business Profile and the pages against what turns up, then measure where the positions actually went. Rome and the towns around it are where the work gets tested first, before it goes to a business anywhere else in the state.

No Layer Between You and the Work

There's no account manager between a client and the work. When you reach out, Eric is the one who reads it. The audit, the Google Business Profile, the pages we build, the citation records we file, and the monthly report all come from him, without a hand off to somebody who has to go check first.

What a Technology Background Changes About the Work

Search marketing came second here. Years of supporting business technology systems came first, healthcare organizations among the businesses served, and that's where the standard on this site comes from.

So the answers a client gets are ones they can check firsthand. A ranking either moved or it held, and the position goes on a public dashboard stamped with the date it was captured. The monthly report can be held against a client's own Search Console the same way.
