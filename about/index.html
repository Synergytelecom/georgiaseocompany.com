Georgia SEO Company Is Led by Eric Kirby

Georgia SEO Company is led by Eric Kirby, based in Rome, Georgia, who has spent more than two decades in business technology, including real, hands-on work inside healthcare IT, where a mistake costs more than a lost click and getting the diagnosis right the first time actually matters. That habit, treat a problem as something to diagnose and fix rather than guess at, carried straight over into how we handle the SEO work under this name. Northwest Georgia is home ground for that reason: Rome and the towns around it are where we test the work first, before offering it to a business anywhere else in the state.

We do not run an account manager layer between a client and the actual work. When you reach out, Eric is the one who reads it, and the audit on a business's Google Business Profile, the pages we build, the citations we file, and the monthly report that goes out all come from him directly, not relayed through someone else who has to go check first.

Why a Technology Background, Not a Marketing One

Search marketing was not the first business built under this roof. Years supporting real business technology systems, healthcare organizations among the clients along the way, came first, and that history shapes a specific habit: a system either does what it is supposed to do, measurably, or it does not, and there is no room for a status update that describes activity instead of an outcome.

SEO work gets held to that same standard here. A ranking either moved or it did not, and the honest answer is always something a client can check for themselves, not something they have to take on faith from a monthly slide deck.

Georgia SEO Company and Synergy Telecom & Medical Systems

Georgia SEO Company is a trade name of Synergy Telecom & Medical Systems LLC, the same company that runs technology services under the Synergy name. The two brands share an owner and a way of working, but they cover different jobs: Synergy Telecom & Medical Systems handles technology services, and Georgia SEO Company is where the SEO and web design work for Georgia businesses lives. Anyone who wants to see the broader technology side of that relationship can look at synergytelecom.org directly; this site is deliberately the SEO and web design half of the same operation.

Nothing about that relationship sits in confusing fine print. It is stated here plainly because a business deciding whether to hire us deserves to know exactly who they would actually be working with, and why a technology company ended up building an SEO practice in the first place.

Frequently asked questions

Who will I actually be talking to if I reach out to Georgia SEO Company?

Eric Kirby. There is no call center or account management layer routing you somewhere else first; he handles the audit, the build, and the monthly report himself, and he is who you would be talking to on day one.

Is Georgia SEO Company the same company as Synergy Telecom?

Yes, in the sense that matters legally: one LLC, Synergy Telecom & Medical Systems, owns both brands. What differs is the work each one is set up to do, covered in the section above, and anyone can check that relationship directly at synergytelecom.org rather than take it on faith.

Why move from healthcare IT and business technology into SEO?

The underlying skill carried over directly: diagnosing what is actually broken in a system before touching anything, then measuring whether the fix worked. SEO turned out to be the same discipline, aimed at search rankings instead of servers and networks.

Do you only work with businesses in Rome?

No. Rome is home base and where the work gets proven first, but the reach extends across Northwest Georgia and out to the rest of Georgia too.
