Georgia SEO Company Is Led by Eric Kirby

Eric Kirby leads Georgia SEO Company from Rome, Georgia. He has spent more than two decades in business technology, including real, hands-on work inside healthcare IT. In that world a wrong diagnosis costs far more than a lost click, and getting it right the first time is the whole job.

That habit carried straight over to the SEO work under this name. Diagnose the problem, fix it, then check whether the fix held. Northwest Georgia is home ground for the same reason: Rome and the towns around it are where we test the work first, before offering it to a business anywhere else in the state.

We do not put an account manager between a client and the actual work. When you reach out, Eric is the one who reads it. The audit on a business's Google Business Profile, the pages we build, the citations we file, and the monthly report all come from him directly, not relayed through someone who has to go check first.

Why a Technology Background, Not a Marketing One

Search marketing was not the first business built under this roof. Years of supporting real business technology systems came first, healthcare organizations among the clients along the way.

That history left a specific habit behind. A system either does what it is supposed to do, measurably, or it does not. A status update that describes activity instead of an outcome does not answer the question.

SEO work is held to that same standard here. A ranking either moved or it did not. The honest answer is always something a client can check firsthand, not something to take on faith from a monthly slide deck.
