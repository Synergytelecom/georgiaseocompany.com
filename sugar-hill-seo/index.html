What a High Bar Costs in Months

The other three searches in the same city look almost nothing like that. Note which side SEO services falls on. The label on a search term is a poor sorter here, and a business that reads every SEO-labeled term as the open lane will scope the wrong job.

What It Takes for Google to Show No Pack at All

A missing pack is a different finding from a thin one. Google needs a minimum number of businesses with complete, category-matched profiles before it will display a three-seat map pack for a given search. When digital marketing agency in Sugar Hill returned nothing, that means the businesses that might otherwise qualify have yet to build a profile specific enough to that category.

That's a different opportunity from closing the 67-to-69 gap, and a faster one. Building the first complete, correctly categorized profile around that phrase takes being the first one Google has enough to show, which is lighter work than outlasting an established competitor. Two situations that far apart sitting on one page is why a citywide average is the wrong number to plan a Sugar Hill engagement around.
