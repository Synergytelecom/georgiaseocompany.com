What We Mean by Digital Marketing

Digital marketing covers every channel a business might use to get found, from paid ads and social to SEO and the website itself. We do the owned half of that list: SEO and web design, the two channels that keep working after the spending stops. That matters most to a business that wants something of its own running alongside paid ads, or to anyone comparing a narrower agency against a full-service one.

The label digital marketing agency can mean paid ads, social media management, email, SEO, web design, or some mix of all five, depending on who you ask. The parts we do are search engine optimization and the website it points to. That is a narrower promise than a full-service digital marketing company makes.

Own What You Build, Rent What You Buy

The distinction that matters is what you own versus what you rent. A paid ad campaign produces traffic exactly as long as the budget keeps flowing, and stops the day it does not. A well-built website with rankings behind it keeps producing traffic on its own, because you own the asset instead of leasing a spot at the top of someone else's auction. Good internet marketing services usually include both. They are not the same kind of investment, though, and a budget that treats them as interchangeable keeps paying for the one that stops the day you stop.

If paid ads, social, or email are also part of your plan, we are glad to work alongside whoever runs those instead of insisting on owning every channel.

A Simple Test for Any Channel

One test for any channel in a marketing budget: if spending on it stopped tomorrow, would a customer still find you next month? For a well-built site with earned rankings, mostly yes. For a paid campaign, no.

What We Deliver

Who This Fits

This is not the right fit if you want one vendor running paid ads, social posting, and SEO from a single contract with a single point of contact for everything. We do the owned half of that stack and say so up front, instead of stretching to cover channels we do not run.

Where it does fit: a business already running paid ads that wants traffic which does not vanish the moment the ad budget pauses, and a business hiring a digital marketing agency for the first time that wants to know exactly what it is buying.

How We Work

Whether we are your only marketing vendor or one of several, the process stays the same:

  1. Audit the owned assets. Current rankings, Business Profile health, and site performance, so we know the starting point.
  2. Build the plan. A scoped set of priorities for SEO and web design, coordinated with whatever paid or social work is already running.
  3. Execute and publish. Fixes, content, and citation work go out on a set schedule, not in an occasional burst.
  4. Report against your numbers. Rankings and traffic reported monthly, in a format you can compare against whatever your paid channels are reporting too.

What Coordinating With Your Ads Vendor Looks Like

Coordinating with a paid ads or social vendor means a few specific things. We share the numbers that overlap both channels, like a jump in branded search traffic a paid campaign may have caused, so nobody reads their own report in isolation and takes credit the other side earned. We coordinate on timing where it matters: holding a content push until a paid campaign for the same offer has run, or flagging when a page we are optimizing is also somebody's ad landing page. That way a change on our side does not quietly break something on theirs.

We do not require access to another vendor's accounts, and we do not ask a client to relay information back and forth between two agencies that will not talk to each other directly. A short recurring check-in tends to work better than either extreme: fully merged reporting that costs more coordination than it is worth, or no contact at all until something breaks. Which one a given relationship needs is usually clear within the first month.

Frequently asked questions

Do you run paid ads as part of your digital marketing services?

No. We focus specifically on SEO and web design, the owned-asset side of digital marketing, and we are glad to coordinate with whoever manages your paid ads rather than taking that work on ourselves.

Why choose SEO over paid ads for a marketing budget?

It is rarely an either-or choice. Paid ads produce traffic immediately but stop the moment the budget does. SEO takes longer to build but keeps producing traffic without ongoing spend once it is in place, which is why most businesses eventually run both.

What is the difference between a digital marketing agency and an SEO company?

A digital marketing agency often bundles paid ads, social, and email alongside SEO. We describe ourselves more narrowly, as a search engine optimization company that also builds the websites those rankings depend on.

Can you work alongside our existing marketing vendor?

Yes. We can coordinate with an existing paid ads or social media vendor, handling the SEO and website side while they handle theirs.

How do you measure whether digital marketing is working?

Ranking position, organic traffic, and Business Profile performance, reported monthly against a documented starting point, not a summary of tasks completed during the month.
