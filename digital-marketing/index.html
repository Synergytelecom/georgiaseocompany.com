Which Half of Digital Marketing Do We Do?

Digital marketing covers every channel a business uses to get found: paid ads, social, email, SEO, and the website itself. We do the owned half of that list, your website and the rankings it earns. Ads stop the day you stop paying for them. A site with rankings behind it keeps working, because you own the asset instead of leasing a spot at the top of somebody else's auction.

So we're a narrow shop on purpose. If you want one vendor running ads, social posting, and SEO under one contract with one point of contact, that's a full-service agency, and we aren't one. We don't run social accounts, post to them, or set them up.

Search work is also the wrong first buy when next week's schedule is empty. A funded ad campaign produces clicks the same week it starts, so an ads specialist is the better first call when the gap is immediate.

The labels are worth pinning down before you compare two quotes. One shop's digital marketing means paid ads and social posting. The next one's means email, a newsletter, and a logo refresh. Ours means two things: the site and the search rankings it earns.

What Our Internet Marketing Services Deliver

Four things, all of them yours at the end of it:

  • Search engine optimization. Google Business Profile work, on-page and technical fixes, citations, and pages, aimed at the map pack and the organic results together.
  • A website built for search. Structure settled before design, so the site can hold the rankings the SEO earns and turn them into calls.
  • Pages aimed at real searches. Keyword research first, then a page for each term your customers are actually typing.
  • Monthly measurement. Rankings, traffic, and profile performance in a format you can set beside whatever your ads vendor reports.

Owned means owned. When an engagement ends you keep the site and its files, access to the Google Business Profile, and the logins for the citation accounts. A paused ad account leaves you with the last invoice.

What a Digital Marketing Company Owes You Every Month

Every month we check your market for changes in keyword demand, and we review other markets and other websites for keyword trends worth moving on early. We optimize the site and the Google Business Profile against what we find, and the report says what moved and what sat still.

You can hold any line in that report against your own Google Search Console, which is free, already yours, and shows the same clicks, impressions, and average position we're reading.

Filter it for the searches that carry your business name and you've split the audience in two: people who already knew who you were, which is where an ad campaign shows up first, and strangers who found you on what you sell, which is what the owned side is for.

Our clients' rankings are on a public results page, which is the kind of record a rented channel never leaves behind.

Working Alongside Your Ads Vendor

If you're already running ads or social with someone else, we work alongside them. That means a few specific things.

We share the numbers that overlap both channels, a jump in branded search a campaign may have caused among them, so nobody reads their own report in isolation and takes credit the other side earned.

We coordinate timing where it matters: holding a content push until a campaign for the same offer has run, or flagging when a page we're optimizing is also somebody's ad landing page.

We don't ask for access to another vendor's accounts, and we don't ask a client to carry messages between two agencies. A short recurring check-in covers it, and which relationships need more than that is clear inside the first month.

Frequently asked questions

Do you run paid ads as part of your digital marketing services?

No. We focus specifically on SEO and web design, the owned-asset side of digital marketing, and we are glad to coordinate with whoever manages your paid ads rather than taking that work on ourselves.

Why choose SEO over paid ads for a marketing budget?

It is rarely an either-or choice. Paid ads produce traffic immediately but stop the moment the budget does. SEO takes longer to build but keeps producing traffic without ongoing spend once it is in place, which is why most businesses eventually run both.

What is the difference between a digital marketing agency and an SEO company?

A digital marketing agency often bundles paid ads, social, and email alongside SEO. We describe ourselves more narrowly, as a search engine optimization company that also builds the websites those rankings depend on.

Can you work alongside our existing marketing vendor?

Yes. We can coordinate with an existing paid ads or social media vendor, handling the SEO and website side while they handle theirs.

How do you measure whether digital marketing is working?

Ranking position, organic traffic, and Business Profile performance, reported monthly against a documented starting point, not a summary of tasks completed during the month.

Paying for Clicks and Owning Nothing Yet?

The question worth answering first is what the owned side would add on top of the ads already running. That starts with a look at what you own today: the site, the profile, and whatever the two of them rank for right now.
