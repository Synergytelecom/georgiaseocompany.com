What We Mean by Digital Marketing

Digital marketing agency is a broad label that can mean paid ads, social media management, email, SEO, web design, or some mix of all five depending on who you ask. We are specific about which parts of that list we do: search engine optimization and the website it points to. That is a narrower promise than a full-service digital marketing company makes, and we think that narrowness is the point.

The distinction that matters is what you own versus what you rent. A paid ad campaign produces traffic exactly as long as the budget keeps flowing, and stops the day it does not. A well-built website with rankings behind it keeps producing traffic on its own, because you own the asset instead of leasing a spot at the top of someone else's auction. Good internet marketing services usually include both, but they are not the same kind of investment, and treating them as interchangeable line items misses that.

We are the owned-asset half of that mix: the SEO and the website. If paid ads, social, or email are also part of your plan, we are glad to work alongside whoever runs those, rather than insisting on owning every channel ourselves.

Who This Fits

This fits a business that already runs paid ads and wants an owned-asset counterweight so traffic does not vanish the moment the ad budget pauses, and it fits a business evaluating a digital marketing agency for the first time that wants to understand exactly what it is buying rather than a bundled package of vague deliverables.

Frequently asked questions

Do you run paid ads as part of your digital marketing services?

No. We focus specifically on SEO and web design, the owned-asset side of digital marketing, and we are glad to coordinate with whoever manages your paid ads rather than taking that work on ourselves.

Why choose SEO over paid ads for a marketing budget?

It is rarely an either-or choice. Paid ads produce traffic immediately but stop the moment the budget does. SEO takes longer to build but keeps producing traffic without ongoing spend once it is in place, which is why most businesses eventually run both.

What is the difference between a digital marketing agency and an SEO company?

A digital marketing agency often bundles paid ads, social, and email alongside SEO. We describe ourselves more narrowly, as a search engine optimization company that also builds the websites those rankings depend on.

Can you work alongside our existing marketing vendor?

Yes. We regularly coordinate with an existing paid ads or social media vendor, handling the SEO and website side while they handle theirs.

How do you measure whether digital marketing is working?

Ranking position, organic traffic, and Business Profile performance, reported monthly against a documented starting point, not a summary of tasks completed during the month.
