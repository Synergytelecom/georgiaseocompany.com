A Claimed Profile Isn't a Finished Profile

A claimed profile is a starting line, and in Chamblee that distinction is most of the job. A typical listing here has been claimed, given a category, and filled in with hours and a service area, and then nobody touched it again. Google has enough to display that business and almost nothing to rank it above a competitor with review history behind them.

What finishes a profile is unglamorous and it never stops: the right primary category, services described the way customers say them, photos that show current work, and reviews arriving steadily instead of in a single burst two years ago. That work is the difference between being listed and being called.

Frequently asked questions

Somebody nearby searched for services near me and my Chamblee business never appeared. Why?

Three settings usually explain it: your primary category, your service area, and how recently you earned a review. Near me searches drop the city name, so Google decides entirely from your profile and the searcher location. A business filed under the wrong category can be a mile away and still lose to one across town.

Do I need a new website, or can you work with the one I have?

Often we can work with it. If the pages load fast enough, the structure is sane, and you can edit it, the money is better spent on the profile and on new pages. A rebuild earns its cost when the site is slow, when it fights you on edits, or when it has one page trying to sell every service you offer.

How long before a Chamblee profile starts moving?

Category and service-area work often shows within two to four weeks. Reviews and page depth take longer, so plan on three to six months for the phrases with real competition. The report each month tells you whether the direction is right long before the ranking arrives.
