Why a Lyerly Search Fills Up With Summerville Names

The way through it is specificity. Setting a Service Radius From a Town This Size

Almost every business in a place like this serves an area many times larger than the town itself, and the coverage area on the profile is where you tell Google how far that goes. Set it from the last few months of real jobs, name Summerville, Trion and Menlo if you truly work them, and leave off the counties you reach twice a year.

Then let it follow the work. Coverage that grows as your routes grow keeps the profile honest, and honest coverage backed by reviews from those same towns is what makes a listing from a small town outrank a bigger one that claimed everything at once and served none of it well.

If you run without a storefront, publish the coverage area and hide the street address. That configuration exists for exactly this kind of business, and using it correctly keeps a home address off the internet while leaving your ability to compete completely intact.
