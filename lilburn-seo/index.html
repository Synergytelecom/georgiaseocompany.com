How to Read a Page Like This One

A page like Lilburn's, with no missing pack and no outlier pulling the numbers around, is the clearest place to see what these figures measure. The review count sitting behind whichever business currently holds the top map pack seat, for one search term, on one day. It is not a prediction and it is not a permanent scoreboard. What a Quarterly Re-Measurement Will Show Here

The plan, per the spec behind this entire fourteen-city band, is to re-run this same single-capture measurement on a quarterly cycle, Lilburn included. What that re-run will show is not a guess. Those are the three things that move a number like this, not chance.

A market this ordinary is, in a sense, the easiest one on this whole list to track meaningfully over time, precisely because nothing here is skewed by a single outlier or a missing pack that could resolve on its own.
