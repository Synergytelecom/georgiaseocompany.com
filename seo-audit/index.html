What a Website SEO Audit Turns Up

It covers the same ground we use to scope every engagement:

  • Current rankings. Where you sit today for your priority terms, in the map pack and in the organic results below it.
  • Google Business Profile review. Category, service area, completeness, and any verification or suspension problem sitting on the listing.
  • Site check. Page speed, mobile usability, and the structural problems that change how a search engine reads the site.
  • Content gaps. Search terms your competitors rank for that your site has no page aimed at.
  • Competitor snapshot. Who holds the spots you want, and what they're doing that you aren't yet.
  • A written summary. The findings in the order of what they're costing you.

How the Audit Runs

Four steps, and the whole thing costs you the time it takes to answer a few questions about the business:

  1. Request. Your website and the town you work in, sent through the contact page.
  2. Data pull. Rankings, Business Profile, and site health, gathered before anybody forms an opinion.
  3. Manual review. A person goes through the data and puts it in order for your business and your market.
  4. Findings call. We walk you through the results and take questions, rather than emailing a PDF and going quiet.

How We Decide What Matters Most

An automated tool treats every flag as equally important, which is why a fifty-item list from one is little use until somebody ranks it. Every issue on your list gets ranked by one test: whether fixing it changes whether you show up for a search that brings you customers. A missing meta description on a page nobody searches for sits below a category mismatch keeping a whole Google Business Profile out of the map pack. A scanner flags both the same way.

Distance to lost business breaks the ties. A contact page that loads slowly costs you the visitor who was ready to call. A page title a few characters too long is one small signal among many.

Speed of the fix counts too. A quick fix with visible impact gets named first, even when a slower project matters more across a year, because a business acting on an audit needs somewhere to start on Monday.

What Happens After the Audit

Plenty of owners take the list somewhere else, and that's a fine outcome. If we do the work, the same checks run every month from then on. We check your market for changes in keyword demand, watch how the site and the profile are performing for the terms your customers use, and optimize both against what we find.

The monthly report sets this month's numbers next to the ones in your audit, so the baseline you started from stays the thing everything gets compared against. Where our clients rank is on our results page.
