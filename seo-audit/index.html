What an SEO Audit Actually Checks

A lot of what gets called a free seo audit online is an automated tool spitting out a score with no context: a number between one and one hundred and a list of generic warnings that apply to almost every website on the internet. That kind of report tells you very little about why you are not showing up for the searches that actually matter to your business.

Our website seo audit is a manual review, not a generated report. We pull your current rankings for the keywords that matter to your business, check your Google Business Profile for category, service area, and completeness issues, and review your site for the technical and on-page problems actually holding it back. Where an automated tool flags fifty minor issues with no priority order, we tell you which three actually matter and why, and we explain the reasoning behind that ranking in plain language rather than handing you a score to take on faith.

This is also where we recommend starting: request one before committing to any ongoing work. It works just as well as a check on a site that is already ranking, since a business doing fine today can still be sitting on gaps that surface the moment a competitor starts investing in the same keywords.

What You Get

The audit covers the same ground we use to scope every client engagement:

  • Current ranking check. Where you actually rank today for your priority keywords, in the map pack and in organic results.
  • Google Business Profile review. Category accuracy, service area setup, completeness, and any suspension or verification issues.
  • Technical site check. Page speed, mobile usability, and structural issues that affect how search engines read the site.
  • Content gap review. Search terms your competitors rank for that your site currently has no page targeting.
  • Competitor snapshot. A look at who currently holds your target rankings and what specifically they are doing differently.
  • Backlink snapshot. A quick look at where your existing links come from and whether anything in the profile looks thin or risky.
  • A written summary. Findings ranked by likely impact, not a raw dump of every issue an automated tool can find.

How the Audit Process Works

Requesting an audit is straightforward, and nothing about it requires a commitment beyond the time it takes to answer a few questions about your business:

  1. Request. Submit your website and location through our contact page, no cost and no obligation attached.
  2. Data pull. We check your current rankings, Business Profile, and site technical health.
  3. Manual review. A person, not just a tool, goes through the data and prioritizes what actually matters for your specific business and market.
  4. Findings call. We walk through the results with you directly and answer questions, rather than emailing a PDF and disappearing.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SEO audit really free?

Yes, the initial audit costs nothing and carries no obligation to hire us for anything further. It is where we recommend starting before anything else.

How long does an SEO audit take?

Most audits are ready within a few business days of request, depending on how much history and competitive data needs to be pulled for your specific market.

No. We pull the data with tools, but a person reviews and prioritizes the findings for your specific business rather than handing you a generated score with no context.

That happens, and we say so directly. Some businesses are already doing the fundamentals right and mainly need patience or a narrower set of fixes rather than a full engagement.

No. The findings are yours to act on however you choose, whether that means hiring us, hiring someone else, or handling the fixes in house.
