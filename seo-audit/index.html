What an SEO Audit Checks

An SEO audit is a manual review of your current rankings, your Google Business Profile, and your website, done to find what is holding your visibility back. Unlike an automated scan, it ranks the handful of issues that matter instead of listing every minor flag a tool can find. It fits any business unsure why it does not show up for searches it should be winning, whether or not there is a budget to act on the findings yet.

A lot of what gets called a free seo audit online is an automated tool spitting out a score with no context, plus a list of generic warnings that apply to almost every website on the internet. That kind of report tells you very little about why you are not showing up for the searches that matter to your business.

Our website SEO audit is a manual review, not a generated report. We pull your current rankings for the keywords that matter to your business, check your Google Business Profile for category, service area, and completeness issues, and review your site for the technical and on-page problems holding it back. Where an automated tool flags fifty minor issues in no particular order, we tell you which three matter and why.

We recommend starting here, before committing to any ongoing work. An audit is just as useful on a site that already ranks, since a business doing fine today can still be sitting on gaps that open up the moment a competitor starts investing in the same keywords.

What You Get

The audit covers the same ground we use to scope every client engagement:

  • Current ranking check. Where you rank today for your priority keywords, in the map pack and in organic results.
  • Google Business Profile review. Category accuracy, service area setup, completeness, and any suspension or verification issues.
  • Technical site check. Page speed, mobile usability, and structural issues that affect how search engines read the site.
  • Content gap review. Search terms your competitors rank for that your site currently has no page targeting.
  • Competitor snapshot. A look at who currently holds your target rankings and what specifically they are doing differently.
  • Backlink snapshot. A quick look at where your existing links come from and whether anything in the profile looks thin or risky.
  • A written summary. Findings ranked by likely impact, not a raw dump of every issue an automated tool can find.

A business is about to pay for a new website and wants to know what the current one is doing right before deciding what to keep. Or a business cannot tell why it is missing from searches it should be winning.

It is useful whether or not you ever hire us for anything further. We would rather you decide from specific findings about your own site than from a sales pitch. How the Audit Process Works

Requesting an audit is straightforward, and nothing about it requires a commitment beyond the time it takes to answer a few questions about your business:

  1. Request. Submit your website and location through our contact page, no cost and no obligation attached.
  2. Data pull. We check your current rankings, Business Profile, and site technical health.
  3. Manual review. A person, not just a tool, goes through the data and prioritizes what matters for your business and market.
  4. Findings call. We walk through the results with you directly and answer questions, rather than emailing a PDF and disappearing.

How We Decide What Matters Most

An automated tool treats every flag as equally important, which is why a fifty-item list from one is not much use on its own.

Does It Change Whether You Show Up

So we put one question to every issue on the list: does fixing this change whether you show up for a search that brings in customers, or does it just tidy up something no visitor would ever notice? A missing meta description on a page nobody searches for matters less than a category mismatch keeping a whole Google Business Profile out of the map pack, even though a scanner flags both the same way.

How Close the Issue Sits to Lost Business

A slow contact page costs you a visitor who was ready to call. A page title a few characters too long is a smaller signal among many.

Speed of the Fix Counts Too

A quick fix with visible impact gets named first, even when a slower project would matter more over a year, because a business acting on an audit needs a place to start, not just a list.

Frequently asked questions

Is the SEO audit really free?

Yes, the initial audit costs nothing and carries no obligation to hire us for anything further. It is where we recommend starting before anything else.

How long does an SEO audit take?

Most audits are ready within a few business days of request, depending on how much history and competitive data needs to be pulled for your specific market.

Will the audit just be an automated report?

No. We pull the data with tools, but a person reviews and prioritizes the findings for your specific business rather than handing you a generated score with no context.

What if the audit finds nothing seriously wrong?

That happens, and we say so directly. Some businesses are already doing the fundamentals right and mainly need patience or a narrower set of fixes rather than a full engagement.

Do you require a contract to act on the audit findings?

No. The findings are yours to act on however you choose, whether that means hiring us, hiring someone else, or handling the fixes in house.
