Why You're Not Showing in the Norcross Map Pack

Start with the four usual causes. The primary category doesn't match the search. The service area or address falls outside the ring Google drew for that query. The listing is unverified, suspended, or duplicated. Or the review history is older and thinner than the businesses holding the seats.

An incomplete profile is the version nobody notices, because the listing still exists and still looks fine to the owner.
