Snellville Breaks the Pattern Found Almost Everywhere Else in This Band

In several other towns in this fourteen-city band, digital marketing agency turned up the highest or one of the highest review counts on the page. In Snellville it grouped with the lower-numbered side of the split, alongside SEO company, SEO services, and Local SEO, while web design company and web designer held the higher figure.

Which local businesses happened to claim and build out a given category first varies town to town, and Snellville's version of that history produced a different grouping than several neighboring towns did.
