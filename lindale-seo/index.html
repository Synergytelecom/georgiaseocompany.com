What Google Does With an Unincorporated Address

Three fields on a Google Business Profile carry most of the weight for an address like this one, and all three usually get set in the first hour and never opened again:

  • Primary category. It decides which searches you're eligible for at all. Nine more can sit underneath it, and not one of them counts for as much as the field that leads.
  • Service area. Drawn off the roads you really drive, which for most businesses at this address means Rome and the south end of Floyd County before it means anything further out.
  • Name, address and phone. One version of each, reading the same everywhere Google checks. Directories copy whatever they run across, so a second version breeds quietly and costs far more to unwind than it cost to get right.

Distance from the person searching decides a great deal in a county this size, and five miles is enough to reorder the cards under a map. That's the ordinary standing work behind our local SEO program, and it's most of the difference between a listing that rings and one that merely exists.

How Far Should a Lindale Service Radius Reach?

Small communities tempt owners into drawing enormous coverage areas on the theory that a wider circle catches more work. It reads as noise, and it thins out the pages on your site at the same time. A radius stretched across four counties tells a search engine nothing about where you're strong.
