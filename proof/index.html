Story One: Junk N' Your Trunk

A junk removal company in Northwest Georgia, running one dump truck and a few partners. When the work started there was no website and no measured position in any town the truck served. The rebuild ran in order: a site with a page for every town the truck serves, then the Google Business Profile set to the right category and service areas, then reviews earned one finished job at a time.

The July 2026 audit checked twenty search terms across twenty-one towns, four hundred twenty data points in one pass. The pack's top spot came back 86 times, spread over 13 of those markets. A short list of services hasn't landed yet, and those positions are published in the story right beside the wins.

We told it as a twelve panel comic, because a ranking chart doesn't show you the truck.
