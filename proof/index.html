What This Section Is

Most agency case studies cannot be checked. No company name, no website, a percentage with no date on it. Every story here names the client, links the client's own site, and carries the date of the audit its numbers came from.

Three rules hold each one together. A story goes up only after the client gives written permission to use their name, their company and their site link. Every figure comes from a dated capture of live searches in named towns, never an estimate. And the gaps get published beside the gains, in the same list.

Story One: Junk N' Your Trunk

A junk removal company in Northwest Georgia, running one dump truck and a few partners. When the work started there was no website and no measured position in any town the truck served. The rebuild ran in order. A site with a page per town, then the Google Business Profile, then a long stretch of waiting.

The July 2026 audit checked twenty service searches across twenty-one towns, four hundred twenty data points in one pass. It came back with first place in the map pack in 86 of them, across 13 separate markets, and a short list of services that have not landed yet. Both halves are in the story.

We told it as a twelve panel comic, because a ranking chart does not show you the truck.
