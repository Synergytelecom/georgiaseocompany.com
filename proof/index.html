Story One: Junk N' Your Trunk

A junk removal company in Northwest Georgia, running one dump truck and a few partners. When the work started there was no website and no position in any town the truck served. The rebuild ran in order: a site with a page for every town the truck serves, then the Google Business Profile set to the right category and service areas, then reviews earned one finished job at a time.

The truck now takes the top map pack spot in 13 Northwest Georgia towns, Cedartown and Rockmart among them. A short list of services hasn't landed yet.
