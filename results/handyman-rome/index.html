How were these handyman positions actually measured?

Every handyman cell here came from a real Google search run in the town it names, so nothing on this page is averaged or projected. Pack results were read to a depth of 20 and organic to 10, so an empty handyman cell says the business sat outside that depth in that town, which is a narrower claim than saying it was never found anywhere. Any single term-and-town pair that was never captured shows a hatch rather than a blank, across all 8 Rome towns, so a gap in the data cannot be read as a handyman that failed to rank.

Why do some handyman cells sit blank or deep?
