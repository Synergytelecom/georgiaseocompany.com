Frequently asked questions

Which Floyd County painting business is this?

How were these painting positions actually measured?

Every painting cell here came from a real Google search run in the town it names, so nothing on this page is averaged or projected. Pack results were read to a depth of 20 and organic to 10, so an empty painting cell says the business sat outside that depth in that town, which is a narrower claim than saying it was never found anywhere. Any single term-and-town pair that was never captured shows a hatch rather than a blank, across all 14 Floyd County towns, so a gap in the data cannot be read as a painting that failed to rank.

Why do some painting cells sit blank or deep?

Because misses get published next to wins. If every painting cell read 1 there would be nothing here worth checking. This client holds 10 top-three placements and 2 at number one across Floyd County, and the towns still unfinished stay on the page so those two figures mean something.

Would a painting business in a different county get the same result?

Not necessarily, and anyone promising a painting these exact numbers is guessing. What a painting faces in Floyd County is not what it faces elsewhere: rival count, review depth and plain distance from the searcher all shift market to market. What does transfer is the method: 20 painting terms measured in the towns your own crews reach, published every month either way.
