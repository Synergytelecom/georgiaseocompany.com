What We Publish, and Where

We publish the rankings of our clients anonymously on a public dashboard, and it's the same dashboard our clients check themselves. Each business is listed by its trade, with no company name attached, and the board carries the date it was last refreshed.

Georgia SEO Company runs under Synergy Telecom & Medical Systems LLC as a trade name, and the dashboard is published there, built from the same data referenced throughout this site.

What We Check, and How Often

Every number on the dashboard is a real measurement with the date it was taken, pulled from a set list of Georgia towns and search terms we re-check every month. Each search pairs a service with a town, the way a customer types it, and the towns run across the top of the grid with their distance from the business on each one. A position of 1 to 3 means the business is showing in the map pack.

Where a business started sits beside where it stands now. A site we built from scratch starts unranked, since it had no rank before it existed, and a business already established when tracking began shows no starting position at all, because its earlier rank is genuinely unknown.

That monthly pass is also the work a client is paying for. We watch the market for changes in keyword demand, optimize the Google Business Profile and the pages against what shows up, and send a report on what moved. The dashboard is the public half of the same measurement.

Frequently asked questions

Whose clients are the numbers on the live dashboard actually measuring?

Client work we handle under Georgia SEO Company. Synergy Telecom & Medical Systems LLC owns both brands, and this one operates under it as a trade name. One company, one set of numbers, one place to check them.

Why isn't the ranking data shown directly on this page instead of linked out?

A copy-pasted number here would go stale the moment the dashboard updates, and a visitor would have no way to tell which version was current. Linking straight to the live source means there's only one number to check, and it's always the current one.

How often do you re-measure rankings?

Every month. The same set list of Georgia towns and search terms gets checked in one dated pass, and the new figures replace the previous ones on the dashboard with their own date attached. That date is what tells you how current a number is.

Will Georgia SEO Company ever have its own separate results page, apart from the shared dashboard?

Likely yes, once this brand's own client roster is large enough to carry its own dedicated numbers. No date is attached to that yet. When it happens, it shows up here dated the same way everything else on this page is.
