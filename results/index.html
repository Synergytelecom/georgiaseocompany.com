Frequently asked questions

Whose clients are the numbers on this board actually measuring?

Client work we handle under Georgia SEO Company. Each business is listed by its trade and county rather than its name, and every position is a real captured result in the town it names, not an average across a region.

Can I see rankings for a business like mine?

Probably. Each client on the board is listed by trade, so a roofer can look at what a roofer is doing and a junk removal company can look at a junk removal company. If nothing there matches your trade, ask, and we'll tell you straight whether we've run a market like yours.

How often do you check where I rank?

Every month. Same towns, same search terms, so one month reads against the last one, and the report tells you which positions moved and which held.

What happens if one of my rankings drops?

It goes in the report with everything else, along with what we think moved it and what gets worked next. A drop usually means a competitor did something, Google reshuffled the results, or demand shifted to a phrase you're not built for yet. All three are fixable, and none of them get fixed by hiding the number.
