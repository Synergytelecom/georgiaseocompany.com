What a Rome Business Is Paying For

The work splits into pieces a Rome owner can point at and check:

  • Primary category. Your Google Business Profile (the listing that produces the map card, and a separate thing from your website) takes one primary category and up to nine more underneath it. The primary one does most of the work in deciding which Rome searches you turn up for, and it's the field most often set once during setup and never opened again.
  • Service area. Which places the profile says it serves. Floyd County plus the towns a truck actually reaches, Lindale and Shannon and Cave Spring among them, beats a wide radius that claims Cartersville and Calhoun when nobody drives there.
  • NAP. Name, address and phone (the three fields directories copy off each other) as they read everywhere a customer or Google runs into them. Rome carries three ZIP codes, 30149, 30161 and 30165, and a Rome postal address is a different thing from an address inside the city limits. Armuchee High School and Coosa High School both take Rome mail and sit outside them.
  • Review requests. Closing a 22-to-2 gap comes down to asking on the day the job finishes, every time, and asking the person who actually called you.
  • Pages written for Rome. Built around what somebody means by seo company rome ga or web design rome ga, and around what happens after the tap. Whatever opens has to load on a phone and give that person a reason to call.
  • A reporting date. The same kind of ranking data behind this page, plus what Search Console's Performance report lists under Queries (the searches that already put your site in front of somebody), sent to you on a date you know in advance.

Proximity does more here than owners expect.
