Google Business Profile Management in Rome, GA

The map pack, meaning the map plus the three business cards that sit above the blue links, is where a Rome search usually gets decided, and the same listing is what somebody sees in Google Maps. Google builds those cards out of your Google Business Profile, and four fields on it do most of the deciding:

  • Primary category. One primary category, plus up to nine more underneath it. The primary one sets which Rome searches you are even eligible for, and it's the field most often chosen at setup and never opened again.
  • Service area. Floyd County plus the towns a truck actually reaches, Lindale and Shannon and Cave Spring among them. A service area drawn to where you really drive holds up better than a wide radius claiming Cartersville and Calhoun.
  • Name, address and phone. Rome carries three ZIP codes, 30149, 30161 and 30165, and a Rome mailing address is a different thing from an address inside the city limits. All three lines have to read the same everywhere Google checks them.
  • Review requests. Asking on the day the job finishes, every time, and asking the person who actually called you.
