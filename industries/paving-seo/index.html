The Asphalt Plant Closes and So Does Paving Season

Hot mix has to leave the plant warm and go down warm, which sets a Georgia paving season running roughly from spring into late fall and shuts the plants supplying the mix for the winter. Sealcoat wants warm dry pavement with a dry night behind it. A whole year of revenue gets compressed into a stretch of months, and an hour of that stretch is worth more than a week in January.

Frequently asked questions

Should a paving company choose Paving contractor or Asphalt contractor as its category?

Either can work, so match it to the sign on the truck and the work that pays. Keep Concrete contractor in a secondary slot when the crew genuinely pours curb and sidewalk, and stay out of General contractor entirely, since it moves the listing into building searches and away from paving ones.

How far ahead of paving season does the search work have to be done?

Far enough that the pages hold position when buyers plan rather than when crews work. Commercial pages want to be settled by fall for spring budgets, and driveway pages by February. A page for parking lot paving usually needs a few months to find its footing, so publishing it in April aims at a season already booked.

What does a paving website need for commercial buyers?

Something a facilities manager can forward to a board without rewriting it. Scope pages for milling and overlay, crack sealing, sealcoating, and striping, license and insurance detail, references, and a clear way to request a bid. A four bullet services page gives that buyer nothing to act on.

How does a paving contractor answer the door to door scam reputation?

With verifiable detail in public. A real address, license and insurance information, photographs of the company owning its own equipment, and a review count that keeps climbing in named towns. Homeowners have been warned about traveling crews for decades, and looking permanent everywhere they check is the whole answer.
