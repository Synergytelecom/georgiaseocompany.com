When Referrals Slow Down, Somebody Is Still Searching

A lot of these businesses run on word of mouth, until a month when it goes quiet. Most of a surveyor's clients need a survey once and never again, and the work arrives through attorneys, agents and builders. A couple books a venue a year ahead, off photographs and a budget they set before calling anybody, and a travel agent competes less with the agency across town than with the booking platforms that hold the first screen of every broad search.

Local business SEO covers the stretches between referrals and backs the referrals up, because somebody who's been handed your name still looks you up before calling.

Small Business SEO Starts With a Category That Fits

The primary category decides which searches a profile can show up for, and outside the trades the right one is easy to miss. A venue weighs Wedding venue against Event venue. A salon picks between Hair salon and Beauty salon, a choice that splits the traffic, and a dealership runs sales, service and financing off one address with a single listing usually trying to represent all three. Then the profile stays alive with photos and posts every month.

SEO for Local Business Websites That Get Compared

Outside the trades, the website tends to decide the sale, because the buyer is comparing. A couple shortlisting venues wants photographs, capacity and what the fee includes. Nobody reads a video company; they watch thirty seconds of the reel and decide. A dealership's vehicle webpages disappear as cars sell, so each one gets pointed at a webpage that lasts, like the model it belonged to, and a travel agent's specialty webpages compete where booking sites answer badly, on anything that takes a judgment call.

When the website is the bottleneck, we rebuild or rework it ourselves, with no second vendor in the middle.

How Local SEO for Small Business Earns Reviews

Your star rating sits on the map card beside two competitors' ratings, and a person picks from those three before anybody's website has finished loading. The businesses that keep a steady count ask at the same point every time, by text with the review link once the customer has left: after a visit for a clinic, the week after the wedding for a venue, and after a sale or a service visit for a dealership. Every review gets a reply, and nothing gets bought, screened before it reaches Google or traded for a discount.

Frequently asked questions

Should a business with no storefront show its address on Google?

Not when the work happens at the customer's place. A business that travels to its customers hides the street address and sets service areas, Google's documented setup for a service-area business, which keeps a home address off the map.

Do you only work with home service businesses?

No. The same monthly work fits a surveyor, a travel agent, a venue, a dealership, an office, a clinic, a salon or a shop, and what changes is the Google category, the webpages the website needs and the timing of the year.
