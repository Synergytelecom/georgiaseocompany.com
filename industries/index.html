What We Do Every Month for a Georgia Trade Business

We check your market every month for changes in keyword demand, and we watch other markets and other trades for keyword trends worth moving on early. We optimize your Google Business Profile and your website for the terms your customers actually use, then track where you rank town by town and report what moved.

What changes from one trade to the next is the category on the profile, the pages the site needs, and the timing. A family clearing a garage on a Saturday afternoon is on a different clock than a builder ordering a boundary survey before a permit application, or a homeowner comparing three roofers a week after a hailstorm. That clock decides what a map pack spot is worth and what it takes to hold one.

Same-Day Trades

When customers call two or three companies inside the same few minutes, review count and recency carry most of the weight. The profile has to look current on the day they look at it. Junk removal, plumbing, and storm work all sell this way.

Big-Ticket, Slow-Shop Trades

When the buyer reads for three weeks first, the site does more of the work: complete service pages, visible credentials, and answers to the technical questions a shopper has. Run the same review push and the same page template across both and you overspend on one while underserving the other.

What Our Industry SEO Services Include

The scope keeps the same shape whatever the trade. What changes is which category, which pages, and which towns.

  • Keyword research for every service you sell and every town you sell it in.
  • Google Business Profile category, services, service area, and hours set, then checked every month.
  • Title tags and meta descriptions written per service page and per town page.
  • Name, address, and phone records matched across the directories and data aggregators Google cross-checks.
  • Photos and profile posts going up monthly, plus a review request routine your crew can actually run.
  • A report each month showing where you rank town by town and what moved since the last one.

If your name, address, or phone differs across those records, Google trusts the business less and the map pack position suffers for it. Most owners set a profile up once and never open it again, and that is the whole opportunity.

Industries We Do SEO For

  • Junk Removal SEO: same-day decisions, high job volume, review velocity as the main lever.
  • Tree Service SEO: emergency storm work next to scheduled trims, two sales on two different clocks.
  • Land Surveyor SEO: infrequent, high-value, researched purchases with a long cycle and heavy referral weight.

  • HVAC SEO: three jobs that behave like three sales, emergency repair, full replacement, and routine maintenance.

  • Contractor SEO: a handful of large jobs a year, where photos work closer to a portfolio than a trust signal.
