What Our SEO Services Cover Every Month

Four things, and you can name all four: the Google Business Profile (the listing that produces your map card, separate from your website), the pages that have to carry the rankings, the name-address-phone records sitting in Apple Business Connect, Bing Places for Business, Yelp and Data Axle, and a monthly report saying which of those moved.

Rankings and calls usually start moving somewhere in the three to six month range. If the phone has to ring this month, put the money into Google Ads or Local Services Ads, which bill by the click or by the lead and start the day you fund them.

What SEO Optimization Services Actually Touch

The profile goes first because it's the cheapest thing to get wrong: primary category, additional categories, service areas, hours, and the photo set, all of it visible inside your own dashboard without help from us.

The website comes second: page titles, meta descriptions, internal links, and the structured data telling Google what kind of business a page describes. Speed gets measured against Core Web Vitals, Google's name for three numbers (Largest Contentful Paint, Interaction to Next Paint, Cumulative Layout Shift) that PageSpeed Insights returns free for any URL you paste in.

Citations are third and dull. One stale suite number in an aggregator gets copied downstream by directories you've never heard of.

Content is fourth and slowest: pages for the services you sell and the towns you sell them in, written for the terms your customers actually search for.

How Do I Check Any of This Myself?

Two free screens cover most of it. Google Search Console reports clicks, impressions, average clickthrough rate and average position for every phrase that brought somebody to your site, one row per phrase. Your Google Business Profile keeps its own performance view, counting calls, website clicks, direction requests, and how many of each came from Search versus Maps.

Read them together. A call placed straight off your map card never lands in Search Console, because nobody clicked a search result to make it, and that gap is why an owner reads one screen, sees flat numbers, and writes off the map pack work.

What Shows Up in the Report Each Month

Three things, the same three every month. Where your priority keywords stand, map pack and organic both, with the movement since last month beside each one. A dated log of the work itself: which citations got fixed, which pages went live, what changed on the profile. And who currently outranks you, with what they're doing that you aren't yet.

If a line in it doesn't square with your own Search Console, you ask, and a person walks you through it on the phone.

Who Keeps the Logins When This Ends

You do. Three things come with you when an engagement ends: access to the Google Business Profile, the website and its files, and the logins for the citation accounts.

Ask the same question of whoever else you're talking to, and ask it before you sign anything.

What a Search for SEO Services Near Me Actually Returns

Type SEO services near me and the results behave differently from almost any other trade you could search. A plumber's results sort hard by proximity, because somebody has to drive to your house.

An agency does the work over a login and a phone call, so what counts is how well the team knows your market. What comes back is a mix: a few genuinely local firms, several national companies running a page built for your city, and a run of listings that sell a package and call it a result.

Proximity still buys you something real, and it is worth being specific about what. A team in your state knows which counties your customers drive between, has seen the same competitors on other accounts, and can sit across a table when the conversation gets hard.

Rankings are a separate question. Ask every firm on that results page for the same thing: current positions in your towns, measured, with the date on them.

What Affordable SEO Services Usually Leave Out

The cheap offers in that same set of results are usually honest about the price and quiet about the scope. The first things to go are the ones that take a person rather than a script: the Google Business Profile category and service area checked by hand every month, a review routine somebody actually runs, and reporting that names positions in named towns.

Affordable SEO services are worth buying when the scope is written down and the reporting is specific enough to argue with. They stop being affordable the month the invoice arrives and nobody can say which town moved.

What Moves the Number on an SEO Agency Quote

Four things move a quote, and you can estimate three yourself.

  • Town count. Ranking in Rome alone is a smaller build than ranking across Floyd, Bartow, Gordon, Polk and Chattooga counties, since every market needs its own page and its own terms.
  • Site condition. Rewriting titles and internal links on a working site is cheaper than rebuilding one that loses visitors on a phone.
  • Profile status. A verified profile on the right primary category is a starting line. A suspension, or a duplicate listing competing with your own, is a job before the job.
  • Review distance. Catching a competitor who has been collecting reviews for five years takes longer than catching one sitting on a handful.

What SEO Won't Fix, and Who to Call

Some of what gets blamed on rankings lives somewhere else, and it's cheaper to find that out in month one.

When the calls are already coming and nobody returns them inside a day, more ranking pours volume into a leak. A phone process or a CRM fixes that one. When your price sits well above your market, ranking first only means more people find out faster.

Some demand lives on a different surface, like bid work routed through general contractors or anything sold across a trade counter. When that's most of your revenue, Google Ads and Local Services Ads reach it faster than we will, and an ads specialist is the better first call.

Frequently asked questions

Is this month to month or a contract?

Month to month. We scope the work in blocks tied to what your site and your profile need, and you can stop after any month.

Does this work for a single location business?

Yes, and usually sooner than for a business running a dozen locations. One profile competing in one town has fewer moving parts, so category and citation fixes show up in the map pack faster.

Can we start with just the Google Business Profile?

Yes. Plenty of engagements open on the profile alone, or on two or three priority pages, and widen once you've seen what the first block moved. Profile-only work is described on our Google Business Profile management page.

What does "affordable SEO services" mean here?

Priced to the size of your market and the condition of your site, so a one-town business with a working website pays less than a five-county business starting from a rebuild. SEO services for a small business in one Georgia town sit at the low end of the ranges on our pricing page.

Is there a difference between an SEO agency and an SEO company?

Not in the work. Shops use the two words interchangeably, and neither one tells you who opens your account each month, which is the part worth asking about. What matters for a small business SEO agency or a large one is the same: whether somebody who knows your towns does the work, and whether the report lines up with your own Search Console.
