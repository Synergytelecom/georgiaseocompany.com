What Gets Settled First in a Web Design Project?

The structure, before anyone designs a screen. Can a Website Redesign Keep the Rankings You Have?

Usually, when the work happens before anything goes offline. Google ties ranking history to specific addresses, so a website redesign starts with an inventory from your own Search Console: every webpage holding impressions, clicks or links from other websites. Each old address then gets a 301 redirect to the closest new webpage, one at a time, since pointing them all at the homepage throws that history away. The words on the webpages carrying your traffic stay, and the new design goes around them.

On launch day the block that kept the unfinished website out of Google comes off, the new sitemap goes to Search Console, and every form gets submitted and every phone link dialed. A brand-new business with no search history has nothing to carry over and can skip this step.

Already on WordPress, or Selling Online?

A WordPress website can usually be fixed where it stands. We set the permalinks so each address reads like its subject, write titles per webpage in whichever SEO plugin is already installed, Yoast or Rank Math, tidy the thin archive webpages a theme makes on its own, and size the images. Updates are the risk to watch: a plugin release can rewrite every title, and a redirect list stored inside one plugin disappears when that plugin does.

On an online store, category webpages tend to carry most of the searchable demand, and the filter combinations that can multiply one category into thousands of addresses get closed off, except the ones worth indexing. Supplier descriptions repeat on every store selling the item, so the products that sell get rewritten in batches. Marketplace listings on Amazon or Etsy are a separate job we don't take on.

Frequently asked questions

Will rankings dip after the new website launches?

They can wobble even when the work was done correctly. A broad dip that recovers is normal settling, while one webpage that drops and stays down usually points at a redirect or content problem worth fixing.

How long does a web design project take?

A standard business website typically takes several weeks from sitemap approval to launch, longer for a larger website with many service and town webpages. We give a specific timeline once we know the webpage count.

What platform do you build on?

We choose the platform for what the website needs to do and what you want to be able to update yourself afterward, rather than defaulting to one system for every client.
