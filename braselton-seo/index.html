Frequently asked questions

Which county should my Braselton listing say I'm in?

Whichever one your physical address actually sits in, and then the service area does the rest of the work. Guessing here creates mismatched directory records, and Google cross-checks those records when it decides how much to trust the listing.

Can one website rank in four different counties?

Yes, with a page for each town you serve and real detail on each of them. One page trying to cover Braselton, Hoschton, Jefferson, and Winder at once ranks for none of them well. Separate pages, each answering a separate search, is how a small site covers a wide footprint.
